1.4 Output Devices






            






   An output device is any device that takes data in the form of electronic
   signals that the computer understands and changes them into data or
   actions outside the computer system.


   The most common output device is a display.  Other examples of output
   devices are:


   °   Printers:  One of the most common output devices.  Printed output can
       be in many forms, from a complex report of hundreds of pages to a
       sales receipt from the supermarket checkout counter.


   °   Computer-produced speech:  In the United States, when you call for
       directory assistance, the computer responds to your push button
       telephone by using computer-produced speech.




   Often an input device and output device are combined in one physical unit.
   You're probably using one right now:  your display station.


   Input devices, output devices, and devices that combine both input and
   output are sometimes referred to as work stations.
