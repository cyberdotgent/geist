1.8 The AS/400 System






            








   The AS/400 system is a powerful computer system designed with a tremendous
   amount of versatility included to make it suitable for a wide range of
   commercial applications.


   The AS/400 system comes in three machine types, each having several models
   with varying amounts of disk and main storage:


   °   9402
   °   9404
   °   9406


   The 9402 and 9404 units are suitable for a department or a small business
   and will fit under a desk.


   The 9406 unit will support a large number of users and is housed in racks.
   A rack is similar to a five-drawer filing cabinet.
