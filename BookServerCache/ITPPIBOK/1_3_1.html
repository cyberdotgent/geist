1.3.1 Installing TPNS


   Installing TPNS involves the following steps:


   1.  Understand the system and installation requirements.  TPNS is
       distributed on three separate tapes.  One is the product tape, which
       installs under VM or MVS.  The other two are online publications
       tapes:  one is for VM only and the other is for MVS only.  Your system
       requirements will vary depending on what system you are running and
       whether you choose to install online publications.


   2.  Follow the specific installation instructions listed in the TPNS
       Program Directory.


   3.  Authorize TPNS to the operating system.


   Refer to "Installing TPNS" for information about completing each of these
   tasks.  The TPNS Program Directory, which comes with the TPNS distribution
   tape, includes the most current information about installing TPNS.
