1.3 Conducting TPNS Tests

 

   The following sections discuss the general sequence you should use during
   TPNS testing and explain how you can use the other books in the TPNS
   library during the test.  The exact sequence you follow during TPNS
   testing depends on how familiar you are with TPNS, the specific needs of
   your testing situation, and the operating procedures used in your
   organization.


   Below is the general sequence of tasks you perform when conducting TPNS
   tests:


   1.  Install TPNS.
   2.  Plan your test.
       a.  Define test objectives.
       b.  Design and write a test plan.
   3.  Configure your system.
   4.  Define the network to be simulated.
   5.  Create the message generation decks.
   6.  Run the test.
   7.  Use TPNS output to analyze the results.


   Note that planning continues throughout the TPNS test.
