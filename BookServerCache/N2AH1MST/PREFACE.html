PREFACE About This Book


   Volumes 1, 2, and 3 of System Messages describe messages issued by most
   components of IBM MVS/ESA (*) System Product (MVS/ESA SP) Version 4.  The
   books help you interpret and respond to the informational, error, and
   diagnostic messages that the system issues during operation.  The three
   books and their messages, identified by the prefixes, are:


   Book                                                     Message Prefixes
   MVS/ESA System Messages, Volume 1, GC28-1656             ADY - IDC


   MVS/ESA System Messages, Volume 2, GC28-1657             IEA - IEE


   MVS/ESA System Messages, Volume 3, GC28-1658             IEF - IXC
(*) MVS/ESA is a registered trademark of the IBM Corporation.
