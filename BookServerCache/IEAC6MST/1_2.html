   IPCS accepts unformatted dumps and traces as input.  It uses members of
   SYS1.PARMLIB to locate control block models and exit routines.  The models
   and routines allow IPCS to format and analyze the contents of the dump or
   trace data set.  IPCS produces three types of output:  symptom strings
   placed in the headers of dump data sets, control block symbols and
   mappings of storage saved in a dump directory, and formatted reports that
   the user can view at a terminal or print.
