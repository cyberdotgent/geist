FRONT_2 About This Guide


   This guide is of interest to anyone who will be using the AS/400 system
   library.  It addresses questions like:


   °   If I know what I want to do, how do I find which manual explains it?


   °   How do I get a book if I need it and don't have it?


   °   What is online information and how do I use it?


   and any more you might have.  Although this guide will not tell you
   everything you want to know about a subject, it will tell you where to
   look for answers in the AS/400 library.
