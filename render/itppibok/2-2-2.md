### 2.2.2 Printing the TPNS Program Directory on VM

To print a copy of the TPNS Program Directory from the distribution product tape in a VM environment, mount the tape and attach the tape drive to your VM ID as device 181. After you enter the CMS commands shown <> below, the TPNS Program Directory will be printed at the printer assigned to your terminal.
