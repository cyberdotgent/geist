### 2.1.3 VM Requirements

The following sections describe the requirements for installing and <> running TPNS in a VM environment. The numbers given below are divided between resources needed for TPNS itself and resources needed for the <> online publications. Since the online publications are optional, those resources do not necessarily apply.- The stand alone product requires 2500 blocks (15 cylinders of 3380 DASD or 20 cylinders of 3350 DASD) to install. A typical user needs about 50 / cylinders of 3380 DASD or 60 cylinders of 3350 DASD to do useful work. If you want to install online publications, you need additional storage. The online publications require 13 cylinders of 3380 DASD and the unformatted source files require 28 cylinders of 3380 DASD.
