## 5.2.1 Step 1:  Determining the Test Objectives <>

<a id="HDRCONXOB"></a>

### Step 1: Determining the Test Objectives

The first step in using TPNS is to determine the objectives for your test. In this example, the high-level objective is to conduct a <> performance test on your system. Specifically, you want to find out how the system performs if a number of terminals in several domains access the same application at the same time. You decide to do this by simulating 60 terminals in three domains logging on to TSO which is located in Domain A.
