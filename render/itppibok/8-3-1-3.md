#### 8\.3\.1\.3 Adding Terminals

The greatest difference between your network definitions and the example will probably be the number of terminals \(TERM, DEV, LU\) that you define\. Some terminal types require more storage than others\. In general, 3270 terminals require the greatest amount of storage, followed by LU7s, 3101s, and 3643s\.

When you add 3270 terminals to your network definition, allow at least 3810 bytes of storage for each 3270 DEV or LU2 half\-session\. This applies to both BSC and SNA terminals\. The 3270 terminals with additional features and larger display sizes require more storage to simulate\.
