## 3.2 What Should You Test

<a id="HDRTESCHA"></a>

How complex is the change How many components will be changed How many transactions or transaction types will be affected by the change <> How many people will be affected by the change How difficult will backup and recovery be after the change What's my past experience with changes of this type <> The answers to these questions will help you decide what types of changes you want to test with TPNS.
