## 8\.6 Running TPNS as a Permanent Task

<a id="HDRRUNPER"></a>

Depending on the method and goals of using TPNS in your organization, you may want to run one TPNS job as a permanent task\. TPNS can support multiple network simulations from multiple consoles or can be reused by one person after another\. For example, you can run small TPNS tests on your production system during normal working hours\. You do not need to dedicate a system to the test\. Although this method of running TPNS would not work for major performance or stress tests, running TPNS as a permanent task could ease bottlenecks and scheduling problems for people developing TPNS scripts or testing applications\.
