### 9\.1\.4 Using Trace Reports

You can use the following two reports to obtain traces of the information

transmitted or received between the TPNS control program and the system under test:

- Line Trace Report
- SIT Trace Report\.

The information contained in these reports can help you to debug your network definition statements and message generation decks\. The interpretation of the trace data can be found in the appropriate communication controller Principle of Operation manual\.

**Note:** You can activate a line trace and a SIT trace on the same line\. You can also activate a line trace on one line and a SIT trace on another line\.
