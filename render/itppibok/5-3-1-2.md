#### 5.3.1.2 Testing Application Development

```text
   <>    You can use TPNS in at least two ways during application development:<>
   By simulating secondary logical units, you can test VTAM applications.<>
   By simulating primary logical units, you can use TPNS to simulate an
   existing application or as a prototype of a new application.
```
