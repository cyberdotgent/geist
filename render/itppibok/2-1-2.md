### 2.1.2 Communications Controller Requirements

If you will be using communication controllers, you must also install the and TPNS CHEAPP (Channel end) and SIOAPP (Start I/O) appendages. These are needed for the EXCP facility to translate virtual addresses to real <BOOK> addresses and are part of the MVS to communication controller I/O.
