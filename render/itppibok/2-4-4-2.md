#### 2.4.4.2 Multiple TPNS Sessions

When multiple sessions of TPNS are in operation, each must specify a unique APPL name for the TPNS side of the operator interface. For example, for two sessions of TPNS, you can start one with the execution

/ param`eter TNIAPPLS=(TPNSSID1,NETV`SIDE) and you can start the other with `TNIAPPLS=(TPNSSID2,NETVSIDE)`. Notice that the APPL name identifying the NetView side of the operator interface (NETVSIDE) is the same for both sessions of TPNS, but the names for the TPNS APPLs (TPNSSID1 and TPNSSID2) are different.
