## 3.3 What Is the Cost of Testing

<a id="HDRTESCOST"></a>

In general, the cost of testing is less than the cost of not testing.<BOOK> The cost of testing includes the expense of test personnel who could be doing other work, test hardware (for example, a communication controller <BOOK> temporarily dedicated to TPNS), and test tools (for example, a TPNS license).
