### 1.3.3 Configuring Your System

["Determining Your System Configuration"](5-0.md) presents four steps you can follow to configure your system. It also describes the types of logical and physical configurations that are used during TPNS tests. Once you have identified the configurations you want to use, you can determine whether you need to use one or more communication controllers. <> If you do, you may need to generate a TPNS control program, modify the existing NCP*, or both. For specific details about how to generate a TPNS <BOOK> <> <> < [SC31-6008> <> <TPNSDF>](#LNK) <> control program or modify an NCP, refer to Defining TPNS Networks. That book also describes the three methods you can use to connect TPNS communication lines and links to the system under test.
