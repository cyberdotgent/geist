### D.1.1 Simulating NetView, NPDA, and NCCF Alerts

Both NPDA and the Hardware Monitor component of the NetView program accumulate and record event, statistical, and alert data from resources in the network. TPNS does not automatically send meaningful data in response to requests for information from these programs, but it can send unsolicited requests from a simulated physical unit to the host. These requests can contain information about events, statistics, or alerts, which in turn are passed to the Command Facility of the NetView program or to NCCF as network operator alerts, control unit alerts, or hardware alerts.
