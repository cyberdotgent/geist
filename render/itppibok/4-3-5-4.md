#### 4.3.5.4 Schedule

The final section of the test plan indicates the schedule to follow <BOOK> during the test. You should indicate when you plan to begin the TPNS test and how long you expect it to take. You may want to include target dates

<> by which you will complete each sample [test. Fi](4-3-5-4.md#SCHED) gure 2 shows an example schedule for a TPNS test that will simulate 60 terminals logging on to TSO.
