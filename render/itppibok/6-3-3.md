### 6\.3\.3 Simulating SNA Subareas and Domains

<a id="HDRNETSUBA"></a>

TPNS can simulate the resources in one or more domains in an SNA multiple\-domain network\. When it simulates a domain, TPNS performs the functions of the host subarea, the NCP subarea, and the physical and logical units associated with these subareas\. TPNS can also simulate only the NCP subarea and its associated resources\. In this case, the simulated subarea is "owned" by a real host in the system under test\.

TPNS simulates the SSCP control functions and the NCP routing functions on a subarea basis, which means that individual subareas are simulated as separate entities\. Therefore, messages that must traverse several simulated subareas are processed by the routing functions in each of the subareas along the route to the destination subarea\. SNA resources simulated by TPNS can only communicate with SNA resources in the system under test\. The simulated resources communicate with real resources over cross\-domain links or channel attachments to an NCP\.
