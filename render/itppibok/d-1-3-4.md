#### D.1.3.4 Response Time Utility <BOOK>

The TPNS Response Time Utility provides response time statistics and graphs for selected terminals or the entire simulated network after the <BOOK> simulation is finished. It provides you with a great deal of flexibility <> in defining the beginning and end of logical transactions, so that you can measure particular responses or track system response statistics over
