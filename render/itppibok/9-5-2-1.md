#### 9\.5\.2\.1 Deciding Whether to Use RSTATS or the Response Time Utility

In most cases, the results from RSTATS should closely resemble the

results from the Response Time Utility because RSTATS processes the same LOG control block immediately after it is written to the log data set\. However, RSTATS calculates response times during TPNS operation, which prohibits some of the more complex processing available when you use the Response Time Utility\. Like the Response Time Utility, RSTATS processes every transmit and receive record\. However, when you use RSTATS, you cannot define logical transactions\. In addition, certain messages are screened and discarded for SNA, 3270, and LU7 devices\.

In general, you use the RSTATS feature when you want to monitor response times during TPNS operation to ensure that messages are being transmitted and received at reasonable rates\. You use the Response Time Utility when you need more detailed statistics about response times after a TPNS test run\.
