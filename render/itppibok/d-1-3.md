### D\.1\.3 Measuring Response Times

One of the most important considerations in a communication network is

the system response time\. You often want to monitor response times and to keep them under certain thresholds set by your local computing center staff\. You can use TPNS with or without other communication network management \(CNM\) products to measure and report response time statistics\. The following sections discuss several ways to do this\.
