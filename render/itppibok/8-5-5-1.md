#### 8.5.5.1 Debugging TPNS Scripts

The TPNS Display Monitor Facility helps speed up TPNS script development <BOOK> by enabling you to watch the message traffic being sent and received by a simulated 3270 terminal. This is very useful when you are trying to get a new TPNS script to work since you can detect errors as they happen. The TPNS Display Monitor Facility helps you detect the following types of errors: Invalid cursor position Incorrect panel format Devices that are no longer generating messages.
