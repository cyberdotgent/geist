### 4\.4\.2 Pointer and Storage Panels

<a id="HDRBRCMDS"></a>

The IPCS Browse option has three kinds of panels:

- The data entry panel, which is previously explained
- The pointer panel
- The storage panel

["Browsing a Dump Data Set" in topic 4\.5](4-5.md) explains how to use these panels\.
