### 1\.2\.5 Other Pieces of IPCS

*PRDMP*: Print dump \(PRDMP\) is a service aid that IPCS replaced in MVS/ESA SP Version 3\. Most of PRDMP's functions are now available through IPCS subcommands and exit routines\. An appendix in *IPCS Command Reference* gives the equivalent PRDMP functions in IPCS\.

*Problem and Data Set Management*: IPCS has facilities to keep track of problems with your system and dump data sets stored on the system\. However, you usually do not need to use these facilities\. The Information/System family of products provides the same function\.
