### 4.5.2 Pointer Panel

dump.

<a id="INPNL"></a>

![Resource 3](3.png)

Figure 4-18. IPCS Dialog Pointer Panel.

[Figure 4-18](3.png) shows the initial display of the pointer panel after you select a dump source and press Enter on the preceding entry panel. (See [Figure 4-17 in topic 4.5.1](4-5-1.md).) A pointer consists of an address, an address space identifier, and a brief remark pertaining to its position. The pointer stack is retained after you end your IPCS session. You can add pointers to the pointer stack. See ["Selecting Words from the](4-5-3-2.md) [Storage Panel" in topic 4.5.3.2](4-5-3-2.md) and the STACK subcommand for more information.
