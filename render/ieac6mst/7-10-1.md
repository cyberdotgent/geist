### 7\.10\.1 FLAG Keyword

The FLAG keyword on the SETDEF subcommand sets the severity level of messages that will appear in the report\. This includes messages from the NOTE subcommand\. For more information, see the SETDEF subcommand in *IPCS Command Reference*\.
