## 3.2 Identifying Areas of Storage

ST| Identifying Areas of Storage | You come across many addresses while analyzing dumps. Sometimes you do | not know where that address points to. IPCS provides a function to | quickly identify the location of an address in a dump and, if you desire, | move directly to that address in the dump. | The WHERE command accepts a dump address as input. It identifies the area | in the dump where the address is located. It can also stack a pointer to | that location in the dump or, in some cases, skip directly to the address. | The WHERE command tells you: | The address space text | The specified address | The name of the area in the dump that was located at the specified | address | The offset into the identified area | The name of the system area containing the specified address
