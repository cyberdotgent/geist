### 4\.7\.1 Splitting the Display Screen

Split\-screen support is available to permit sharing of the screen by related data\. The data can be areas of the same dump or related areas in different dumps\.

For example, the pointer stack on the pointer panel for a dump can be updated from multiple ISPF logical screens\. Pointer stack update requests are processed in the order of logical screen requests\. Updates to the pointer panel on one logical screen are not reflected on other logical screens until the next transaction is processed on the other logical screens\.
