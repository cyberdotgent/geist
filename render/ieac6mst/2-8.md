## 2.8 Initializing Dumps and Traces

<a id="HDRDINI"></a>

After identifying a dump data set to IPCS through the SETDEF subcommand, you can access the source by entering any subcommand that accesses data in the dump or trace data set. Entering a subcommand causes IPCS to initialize the dump or trace.
