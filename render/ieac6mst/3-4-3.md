## 3.4.3 Get Information About IPCS Subcommands

<a id="HDRGETINFO"></a>

To get information about the syntax, function, and operands of an IPCS subcommand, enter the HELP subcommand. During an IPCS session, you can use the HELP subcommand to receive information explaining the use of any IPCS subcommand. IPCS displays the information at your terminal. To receive a list of all the IPCS subcommands along with a description of each, enter the HELP subcommand as follows:

hel`p` You can also get information on a specific subcommand by entering a subcommand name as an operand on the HELP subcommand. For example, to display a summary of the syntax, function, and operands of the COMCHECK subcommand, enter: `help` `comcheck`

**Note:** To enter the HELP subcommand from the IPCS dialog, you must enter

the abbreviated form of this subcommand, **H**, or use the IPCS subcommand in front of the HELP subcommand as follows: `COMMAND` `===>ipcs` `help` `comcheck` While viewing the help information, press enter to display the next screen of the help. If you need to review the information, enter the HELP subcommand again.
