## 3.4.3 Get Information About IPCS Subcommands

<a id="HDRGETINFO"></a>

IPCS subcommand. IPCS displays the information at your terminal. To receive a list of all the IPCS subcommands along with a description of each, enter the HELP subcommand as follows: hel`p` You can also get information on a specific subcommand by entering a subcommand name as an operand on the HELP subcommand. For example, to display a summary of the syntax, function, and operands of the COMCHECK subcommand, enter: hel`p co`m`check`

**Note:** To enter the HELP subcommand from the IPCS dialog, you must enter

the abbreviated form of this subcommand, **H**, or use the IPCS subcommand in front of the HELP subcommand as follows: COM`MAND ==`=`>ipcs he`l`p co`m`check` While viewing the help information, press enter to display the next screen of the help. If you need to review the information, enter the HELP subcommand again.
