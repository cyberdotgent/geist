### 5\.1\.1 Determining the Appropriate Size for the Dump Directory

<a id="HDRSIZEDD"></a>

The DASD space required by this cluster varies according to the number of dumps represented in it, the size of each one, and the amount of information the directory contains for each dump\.

For most processing, allocate a dump directory with a number of tracks equal to 4 to 6% of the total number of tracks in the dumps to be represented in that directory\. Extensive use of symbol tables and storage maps requires increases in the space required by your dump directory\.
