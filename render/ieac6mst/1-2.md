## 1\.2 IPCS Concepts

[Figure 1\-1](1.png) gives an overview of how IPCS works\.

<a id="FIGOVWIPCS"></a>

![PICTURE 1](1.png)

*Figure 1\-1\. Overview of IPCS*

IPCS accepts unformatted dumps and traces as input\. It uses members of SYS1\.PARMLIB to locate control block models and exit routines\. The models and routines allow IPCS to format and analyze the contents of the dump or trace data set\. IPCS produces three types of output: symptom strings placed in the headers of dump data sets, control block symbols and mappings of storage saved in a dump directory, and formatted reports that the user can view at a terminal or print\.
