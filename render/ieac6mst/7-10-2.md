### 7.10.2 NOSUMMARY Keyword

Several subcommands use the NOSUMMARY keyword to control messages that you | want to hide from the output. For example, the DROPDUMP subcommand issues | the message "All records for 1 dump dropped" unless you specify NOSUMMARY.
