## 4.6.3 Help Reading Formatted Dump Reports

<a id="HDRHELPDDR"></a>

While reading a formatted dump report on the dump display reporter panel, press PF1 or enter HELP on the command line to see the tutorial on viewing IPCS reports. On this tutorial panel, options 1 through 5 explain how to use the dump display reporter panel. Option 6, if available, gives information on reading the formatted dump report produced by the

subcommand you entered.
