### 7.6.3 Printing Dump Storage

Usually you will use IPCS subcommands in REXX execs and CLISTs to format | and analyze dump storage. However, you can add unformatted sections of | dump storage to your dump report. IPCS provides a set of CLISTs in | SYS1.SBLSCLI0 that can be invoked from your CLIST to print sections of | storage. The following table summarizes these CLISTs:

<a id="TBLUNIQ27"></a>

<a id="TBLTBLUNIQ27"></a>

| \| | CLIST<br>BLSCPCSA | System storage printed<br>The common storage area (CSA) and extended CSA (ESCA) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BLSCPNUC | The nucleus, extended nucleus, read-only nucleus, and | . \| |
| BLSCPSQA | The global system queue area (SQA) and extended SQA |  |


For an example of how to invoke these CLISTs from a CLIST, see the | BLSCPRNT CLIST in SYS1.SBLSCLI0.
