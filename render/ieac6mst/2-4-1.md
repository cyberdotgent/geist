### 2\.4\.1 Invoking ISPF under IPCS

<a id="HDRSPFOOPS"></a>

Do **not** use the TSO subcommand when you invoke ISPF from IPCS line mode\. Instead, ISPF should be invoked as an IPCS subcommand\. For example, to invoke ISPF from IPCS line mode, enter "ISPF" after the IPCS prompt:

`ISPF`

If ISPF is invoked in any other way \(such as TSO ISPF\), variable processing produces unpredictable results\.

In addition, do not include ISPF and IPCS in the TSO/E authorized command table\.
