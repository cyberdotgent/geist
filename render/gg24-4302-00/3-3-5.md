### 3\.3\.5 Value

Clearly, the WLM will be the only way in the future of allocating system resources\. Its role will be crucial in managing the work distribution across Parallel Sysplexes\. But even on a single MVS system, it can provide significant benefit\.

The WLM way of defining the business importance of transactions and batch work is simpler and less error prone than before and consequently more likely to achieve the desired results\.

Using RMF, it is very easy to monitor performance against goals\.
