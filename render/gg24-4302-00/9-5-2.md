### 9.5.2 Items with Fallback Implications

```text
   The following IMS 5.1 functions do not have a direct counterpart in
   earlier IMS releases.  In case of fallback the prior IMS system must be
   restored and able to operate as it did before the IMS 5.1 conversion:

   ° MSDB to DEDB (probably VSO) conversion
   ° MVS Work Load Manager

   ° /PSTOP LINK FORCE for hung MSC links.
```
