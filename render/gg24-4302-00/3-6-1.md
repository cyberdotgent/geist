### 3.6.1 Overview

Routine (DFSERA70): Standard fixe*d for*mat for prints of database log records All *log* records associated with a specified unit of recovery (UOR) can be printed. These changes will simplify the analysis of problems or issues relating to database updates or complete UORs.
