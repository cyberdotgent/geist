### 3.7.1 Overview

```text
   Some IMS customers (DBCTL and Transaction Manager) are still experiencing
   virtual storage constraint in the MVS CSA.  To provide further relief, IMS (    5.1 has moved a number of modules from CSA into Extended CSA (ECSA),
   reducing the CSA requirement by about 100KB.
```
