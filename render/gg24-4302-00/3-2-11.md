### 3.2.11 DEDB High Speed Reorganization Utility

called the DEDB Direct Reorganization Utility. It has been rewritten and given a new name that reflects one of its new characteristics, namely,

DEDB **High** **Speed** Reorganization Utility. The objectives of the new utility are twofold: To increase performance by exploiting data-in-memory techniques, reducing I/Os, and using asynchronous rather than synchronous I/Os and to reduce the amount of log data produced..
