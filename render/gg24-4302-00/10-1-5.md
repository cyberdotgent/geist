### 10.1.5 Access to DEDBs

The benefits of DEDBs are: Very high performance Very high availability Support for very large databases. These benefits are available to CICS DBCTL users.( With IMS 5.1, CICS can also make use of the in-memory facility for DEDBs provided by the VSO.
