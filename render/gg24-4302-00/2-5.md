## Discontinued Support

before migrating to IMS 5.1. The LU6.1 adapter for LU6.2 applications will not be supported in IMS 5.1. The adapter is shipped with IMS 5.1 and can be used on an unsupported basis at the customer's own risk. This may ease the migration burden for some customers; in general a conversion to APPC/IMS is recommended before migrating to IMS 5.1.
