### 7.1.2 Migration Considerations

```text
   Because online authorization was not requested for VSAM databases
   (including DEDB) in earlier versions of IMS, it would be a wise precaution
   before moving to IMS 5.1 to check that the database data sets are defined
   to RACF, the IMS control region and DL/1 SAS have been assigned userids,
   and these userids are authorized to access the data sets.
```
