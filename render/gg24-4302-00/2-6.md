## 2.6 Discontinued Support

For a long time now, IBM has indicated that CICS Local DL/1 would not be supported after IMS 4.1. All CICS DL/1 users must convert to using DBCTL before migrating to IMS 5.1. The LU6.1 adapter for LU6.2 applications will not be supported in IMS 5.1. The adapter is shipped with IMS 5.1 and can be used on an unsupported basis at the customer's own risk. This may ease the migration burden for some customers; in general a conversion to APPC/IMS is recommended before migrating to IMS 5.1.
