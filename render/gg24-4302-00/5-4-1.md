### 5.4.1 Overview

```text
   From time to time, many MSC users have had problems associated with VTAM
   errors that cause VTAM and IMS to have a different perception of a link's
   status. In these cases, the link is unusable without an IMS restart.

   IMS 5.1 removes the need to restart IMS.
```
