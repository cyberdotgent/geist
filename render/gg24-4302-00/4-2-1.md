### 4.2.1 Overview

There are many times when the IMS operator needs to perform some function (/DBR, /START, etc) on a group of databases or DEDB areas. This may require that multiple commands be entered. The resulting wait while the commands process can be inconvenient, and the stream of messages while the databases complete processing is generally of no value and might hide a genuinely important message. All these issues are addressed in IMS 5.1.
