### 2.4.2 POSIX Compliance

IBM has chosen to integrate the open interfaces for systems services into the MVS operating system. IBM OpenEdition Services for MVS/ESA V4R3 now provides a common set of system support services based on a series of Portable Operating Systems Interface (POSIX) standards. Because IMS exploits the facilities of MVS, it will get these POSIX services without additional changes to IMS. In other words, POSIX standards are now supported in IMS applications.
