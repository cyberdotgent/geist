### APPENDIX1\.1\.1\.20 Time

<a id="HDRTME"></a>

- The local time zone and daylight saving time zone are set by the current locale\. There are two entries in the C locale: the time zone difference TZDIFF; the time zone name TNAME\. See [Chapter 11,](3-5.md) ["International Locale Support" in topic 3\.5](3-5.md) for more information on specifying the time zone\.
- The era for the `clock()` function starts when the program is started by either a call from the operating system or a call to `system()`\.
