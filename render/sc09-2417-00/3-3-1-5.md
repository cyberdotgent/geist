### 3\.3\.1\.5 Binary Stream Functions

<a id="HDRBSFUNC"></a>

To process AS/400 display files, subfiles, ICF, and printer files you can use `fclose()`, `fopen()`, `fread()` \(not for printer files\), `freopen()`, and `fwrite()`\.
