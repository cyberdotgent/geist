## PREFACE.4 How to Get Help

<a id="HDRHLP"></a>

There are three kinds of online information available to you while you are using VisualAge for C++ for AS/400:


****Online** do**cuments These are complete documents, like the one you are reading now, presented online. These documents contain detailed information on the different aspects of VisualAge for C++ for AS/400. For your convenience, the online documents are presented in a standard format.
