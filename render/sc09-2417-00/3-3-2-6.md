### 3\.3\.2\.6 Binary Stream Functions

<a id="HDRFBSF"></a>

To process the AS/400 tape files, diskette files, or save files you can use `fclose()`, `fopen()`, `fread()`, `freopen()`, and `fwrite()`\.
