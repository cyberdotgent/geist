# 1\.0 Introduction to BookServer

<a id="HDRINTRO"></a>

<pre>
   IBM BookManager BookServer is a specialized World Wide Web server that
   enables customers to provide libraries of BookManager books, bookshelves
   and bookcases via the Internet World Wide Web.  It is the centerpiece
   component of the IBM BookManager family of products.
   The past several years have seen dramatic growth in the use of the
   Internet, and a corresponding increase in the power and sophistication of
   the tools associated with its use.
   In particular a strong trend towards the use of the Internet as a medium
   for commercial electronic publishing has developed.  This has provided an
   opportunity for IBM Electronic Publishing to develop a facility to enable
   the use of BookManager libraries for such purposes.  The same facility may
   also be used for providing in-house electronic publication via internal
   corporate IP networks, further enlarging the opportunity.
