### 1\.4\.1 External pictures

IBM BookManager BookServer is able to present pictures that are built into an electronic book file, as are all of the BookManager READ products\. [Figure 2](2.png) is an example of this\.

An exciting new capability is the ability to present external pictures as part of the book's content\.

An external picture may be presented either in\-line \(see [Figure 3](1-4-1.md#FIGMONET1)\), or be the target of a [highlighted link](</bookmgr/monetley.jpg>), and may be in any format supported by the reader's WWW browser\.
