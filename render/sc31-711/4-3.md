## 4\.3 SNMP Bridge Traps

<a id="HDRSNBRTRP"></a>

This section lists the traps that can be sent by the SNMP bridge application, and the actions that LNM for AIX takes when these traps are received\. The traps listed are broken down into categories that indicate the agents that generated the traps\. The following kinds of traps are listed here:

- Generic traps
- Bridge\-generated traps
- RFC1286 redirected traps
- 6611 redirected traps
- Frame Relay redirected traps
