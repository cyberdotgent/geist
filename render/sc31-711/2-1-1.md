### 2\.1\.1 Displaying LNM for AIX Status Information

<a id="HDRPRLNMS"></a>

The **lnmstatus** command reports the status of each LNM for AIX daemon configured to operate\. You can use the **lnmstatus** command to determine the current status of the LNM for AIX daemons, their process identifiers \(PIDs\), and their exit statuses\. For specific information about the possible exit statuses that can be returned by the **lnmstatus** command, refer to the man page\.
