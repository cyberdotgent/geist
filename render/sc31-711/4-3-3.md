### 4\.3\.3 RFC1286 Redirected Traps

<a id="MSG"></a>

This section lists the RFC1286 traps that are generated by the agent and then resent by the bridge application\. These traps are defined under the 1\.3\.6\.1\.4\.1\.2\.6\.21\.3\.1 enterprise ID, which is associated with LNM for AIX\. The traps are listed according to specific trap number and are sent to AIX NetView/6000\.

<a id="MSG 1"></a>

- **1** — **Description:** newRoot **LNM** **for** **AIX** **Response:** Delete the old subnet and rediscover the network\.

<a id="MSG 2"></a>

- **2** — **Description:** topologyChange **LNM** **for** **AIX** **Response:** Delete the old subnet and rediscover the network\.
