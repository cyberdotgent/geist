### 2\.1\.4 Clearing LNM for AIX Databases

Clearing the LNM for AIX databases is necessary to resolve certain problems\. However, before you clear the LNM for AIX databases:

- Stop all LNM for AIX applications with the **lnmstop** command\.
- Ensure the AIX NetView/6000 gtmd and ovwdb processes are running\.

Clear the LNM for AIX databases in the following situations:

- After installing a AIX NetView/6000 PTF\.
- If there is an abnormal termination of the lnmtopod process\.
- If there is an abnormal termination of the AIX NetView/6000 gtmd or ovwdb processes\. If one of these processes is abnormally terminated, follow these steps before clearing the databases:

**1\.** Stop the AIX NetView/6000 graphical interface\.

**2\.** Issue the **ovstop** command\.

**3\.** Issue the **ovstart** command\.

If any of the other AIX NetView/6000 processes are abnormally terminated, it is also recommended that you follow the previous steps before clearing the databases\.

For more information about clearing the LNM for AIX databases and for specific instructions on how to do so, read *Getting Started with LAN Network Manager for AIX*\.
