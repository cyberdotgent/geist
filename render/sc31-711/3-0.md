# 3\.0 Chapter 3\. Understanding Logs, Traps, and Filters

<a id="HDRFILTRP"></a>

In addition to graphically displaying the configuration and status of your LAN, LNM for AIX uses the nettl and trapd logs provided by AIX NetView/6000 to record notifications from the LNM for AIX applications and agents\. The nettl command is used to turn logging on and off\. When nettl logging is turned on, errors encountered by AIX NetView/6000 and the products that run with it, such as LNM for AIX, are documented in the nettl log\. The messages logged in the nettl log can indicate such conditions as an agent that cannot be discovered or an error that has occurred with a specific LNM for AIX application\.

AIX NetView/6000 receives and logs all traps from the network and then forwards specific traps from LNM for AIX agents to LNM for AIX\. LNM for AIX processes these traps \(with a few exceptions\) and sends them back to AIX NetView/6000, where they are logged with the traps from other programs, such as Systems Monitor/6000\. To improve your troubleshooting efficiency, LNM for AIX provides correlated trap information to AIX NetView/6000\.

This chapter describes how AIX NetView/6000 logs are used by LNM for AIX, how LNM for AIX handles traps, and how filters are used to ensure that traps are processed correctly\.
