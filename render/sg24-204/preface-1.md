## PREFACE\.1 The Team That Wrote This Redbook

This redbook was produced by a team of specialists from around the world working at the International Technical Support Organization Böblingen Center\.

**Jo Arao Ramos** is Technical Support Manager at TEKA, in Blumenau, Brazil, responsible for VSE/ESA, CICS/VSE and related product areas\.

**Dieter Fähndrich** is Availability Manager working for IBM Germany in Cologne\. He supports products of the CICS family\.

**Heinz Bartling** is instructor for CICS and MQSeries in the IBM Education Center in Mainz\.

**Ulrich Kettner** was the ITSO project leader\. He is a member of the International Technical Support Organization, Böblingen Center\. He writes extensively and teaches IBM classes worldwide on areas dealing with CICS/VSE and networking solutions involving VSE/ESA and CICS/VSE\.
