### 3\.2\.10 VSAM Cluster Definitions

We copied the skeleton SKAPPN from library 59\. This job defines VTAM clusters for APPN connections and activates labels\. It is necessary to supply volume names on which we have VSAM space available, and the respective catalog, in our case the user catalog VSESPUC\.
