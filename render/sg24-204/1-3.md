## 1\.3 CICS Clients and Servers Communicate\!

Combining CICS clients and CICS servers enables you to set up an environment which you can use to distribute data and processing according to your business needs across a wide range of platforms\. Your workstation users can gain access to server resources easily\.

CICS supplies the functionality required to communicate and it supports a wide range of communication protocols\.

As mentioned above, CICS servers and Transaction Servers offer the ISC facilities to allow CICS programs running under their control to communicate with each other\. CICS clients allow non\-CICS programs to communicate using the ECI and EPI functions\. You will find details about ISC and ECI and EPI below\.
