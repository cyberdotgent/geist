### 6\.5\.2 CICS Client Connected to a CICS OS/2 Server

The Security Manager used by CICS OS/2 is defined in the SIT \(SNT or UPM\)\. Valid user IDs/passwords are defined either in CICS OS/2 SNT or OS/2 UPM or CM/2 in case of an APPC LU6\.2 link\.
