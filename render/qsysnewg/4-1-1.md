### 4.1.1 Basic Assistance Level

This assistance level uses the type of display that provides the most | assistance. It supports the most common AS/400 tasks without the use of a | lot of computer terminology, or extra options and functions that you | probably wouldn't use anyway. | For example, when performing a printing task, there are only a few options | you may need to select, such as the printer you want to use or the number | of copies you want printed.
