## 7\.2 Inquiry Messages

An inquiry message needs a reply from you\. It may also contain information, but it is different from an informational message because you have to make some kind of response to it\.

For example, you are writing a report with another person in your department whose work station is on another floor\. You need some information from your partner for your section of the report, so you send her a message through the system:

`===> FRIEDA: Frieda, What in the world is part #45920AC, "doughnut hole size adjustment screw?" From . . . : Norbert 10/31/90 23:59:30`

This is an inquiry message, and unlike the first examples, it requires a reply\.
