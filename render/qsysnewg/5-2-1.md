### 5\.2\.1 Changing Your Password

Occasionally, you will need to change your password\. To do so, simply follow the steps listed below\.

1\. First, get to the AS/400 Operational Assistant menu\.

2\. Then, select option 5 \(Change your password\)\.

The entry display that appears is unusual in that there are no actual lines to type on\. The cursor is just kind of hanging out there in mid air, ready for you to enter your password\.

If you think back to [Chapter 2, "Signing On and Signing Off Your](2-0.md) [Computer,"](2-0.md) you'll remember that your password is never shown on the display for security reasons\.

3\. So even though it won't show up, type in your present \(current\) password\. Move to the next invisible line with your Field Exit key\.

4\. Type your new password and press the Field Exit key again\. Type your new password again for verification\. The system compares the two to be certain you haven't made an error in typing your new password\. Press the Enter key, and you have a new, secret identity\.

<a id="FIGFIGUNIQ59"></a>

![PICTURE 54](54.png)

A password can be a maximum of 10 characters\. The characters can be any letter \(A through Z\), any number \(0 through 9\), and special characters pound \(\#\), dollar \($\), at \(@\), or underscore \(\_\)\. The first character must be a letter or any special character other than an underscore\. There are many things that can affect what you are able to type as your password\. If the system won't accept your new password, check with your system administrator\.
