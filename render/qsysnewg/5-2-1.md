### 5.2.1 Changing Your Password

| follow the steps listed below. | 1. First, get to the AS/400 Operational Assistant menu. | 2. Then, select option 5 (Change your password). | The entry display that appears is unusual in that there are no actual | lines to type on. The cursor is just kind of hanging out there in mid | air, ready for you to enter your password. If you think back to [Chapter 2, "Signing On and Signing Off Your](2-0.md) Comp [uter," you](2-0.md)'ll remember that your password is never shown on the display for security reasons. 3. So even though it won't show up, type in your present (current) | password. Move to the next invisible line with your Field Exit key. | 4. Type your new password and press the Field Exit key again. Type your | new password again for verification. The system compares the two to | be certain you haven't made an error in typing your new password. Press the Enter key, and you have a new, secret identity.
