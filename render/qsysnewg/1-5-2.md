### 1.5.2 Magnetic Disks

<a id="FIGUNIQ6"></a>

![Resource 6](6.png)

PICTURE 6.

Magnetic disks are slower than main storage, but they are still fast. The data on a magnetic disk remains there unless it is replaced by other data. Most data and programs on a computer system are stored on magnetic disk. Usually, magnetic disks cannot be removed from the system. it is called a **disk** **drive**.
