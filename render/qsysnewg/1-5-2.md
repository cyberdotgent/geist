### 1\.5\.2 Magnetic Disks

<a id="FIGFIGUNIQ6"></a>

![PICTURE 6](6.png)

Magnetic disks are slower than main storage, but they are still fast\. The data on a magnetic disk remains there unless it is replaced by other data\. Most data and programs on a computer system are stored on magnetic disk\. Usually, magnetic disks cannot be removed from the system\. Data is written in magnetic disk tracks much like songs are recorded as grooved patterns on the record's surface\. The device that writes onto a magnetic disk and reads data from it is called a **disk drive**\.
