## 3.1 Function Keys

| There are two types of function keys on your keyboard:

| Engraved function keys | Numbered function keys | Engraved function keys are similar to typewriter keys; they generally | perform a single function. Numbered function keys are more powerful; they | can perform a variety of functions, depending on your current task. Let's | take a closer look at both types.
