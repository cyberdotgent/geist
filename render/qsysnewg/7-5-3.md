### 7\.5\.3 Work with Messages Display

Messages are usually held in your message queue\. Messages in your user message queue and your work station message queue can be viewed from the Work with Messages display\. To get to the Work with Messages display, select option **3** from the AS/400 Operational Assistant menu\. All messages \(including interrupt user messages\) are held in your message queue until you delete them\.
