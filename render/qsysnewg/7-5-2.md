### 7.5.2 Break Message Display

Some messages appear directly on your display and have priority over any | other work you are doing. Messages of this kind, which temporarily

interrupt the display station user, are referred to as **break** **messages**. The system operator may send break messages to inform you of critical system events that could affect you. Whenever you receive a break message, your current display is saved and the break message display appears. A break message can be either an informational or an inquiry message. After you examine the message and reply (if necessary), you can return to your original display by pressing the Enter key. You then can resume working at the point where you were interrupted. (Depending on your typing speed, you may have lost a few characters due to the interruption).
