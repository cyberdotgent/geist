#### 2.1 Let's Go

| If your display station screen is blank, find the power switch and switch | it on. | After a few seconds, the **Sign** **On** display comes on.

<a id="FIGUNIQ13"></a>

```text
|                                    Sign On|
|                                                System  . . . . . :   XXXXXXXX|
|                                                Subsystem . . . . :   QBASE|
|                                                Display . . . . . :   DSP01|
|                 User  . . . . . . . . . . . . . .   __________|
|                 Password  . . . . . . . . . . . .|
|                 Program/procedure . . . . . . . .   __________|
|                 Menu  . . . . . . . . . . . . . .   __________|
|                 Current library . . . . . . . . .   __________|
|                                         (C) COPYRIGHT IBM CORP. 1980, 1991.|
```

You can ignore the information in the upper right corner of the display. Only two lines are important to you now, the **User** line and the **Password** line. | **Note:** If the word **password** does not appear on your display, it's because | the security system is set so that none is needed. If this is the | case, you can ignore any password information given in this book.
