## 5.2 Entry Displays

An entry display **prompts** or asks you to type information. Because the form and quantity of this information varies depending on what the display is trying to do, entry displays can appear in many forms. All entry displays have one purpose: they request the user to enter | information that is appropriate to the type of display and the desired | action. One of the neat things about Operational Assistant is that most | of the entry fields are already filled in for you. | The Change Printer Output display is an example of an entry display. Just | for practice, why don't you try getting to this display: | From the Operational Assistant menu, select... | Optio**n** 1 (Work with printer output) and press the Enter key, then... | Move your cursor to the first printer output, now... | Typ**e** 2 in the option field next to the printer output, QSYSPRT, | Press the Enter key and... | You're at the Change Printer Output display!
