# 2.0 Chapter 2.  Signing on and Signing Off Your Computer

<a id="HDRSIGNON"></a>

<a id="FIGUNIQ12"></a>

![Resource 12](12.png)

PICTURE 12.

Before you can use the AS/400 system you must sign on or tell your computer who you are. Not just anyone can sit down at your display

station and use the computer. You have a unique **user** **name** which

identifies you and a secret **password** which excludes imposters from using your identity.

**If...** You haven't been given a user name or a password... **Then...** 1. Ask the person who is in charge of system security for them. That person might be the office manager, a company administrator, or the owner of the business. 2. If you are that person, you'll find the information you need in the *Security* *Concepts* *and* *Planning* manual and the *Operator's* *Guide*.
