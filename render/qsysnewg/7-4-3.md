### 7\.4\.3 User Messages

Any message that comes from another person, including the system operator, is a user message\. You might get a message from the system operator like this one:

`System printer will not be available until 3:00 p.m. Special forms in use. From . . . : SYSOPR 11/01/90 11:32:21`

\.

Or, you might get one from someone else:

`Can you do the accounts receivable on Saturday Bertha is having a baby. From . . . : CLARADUK 11/01/90 11:33:35`

\.
