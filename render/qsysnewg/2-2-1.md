### 2\.2\.1 What If You Make a Typing Mistake

If you make a mistake while typing your name, you can easily correct it\. Find the **Backspace** key located near the upper right corner of your keyboard\. \(The appearance and exact location of the Backspace key depends on which keyboard you have; this will be explained in the next chapter\.\)

![PICTURE 13](13.png)

When you press the Backspace key, the cursor moves to the left one space\. You then can type right over the wrong spelling to correct it\. If there are extra letters left on the line, you can erase them by putting the cursor under them and pressing the long **spacebar** at the bottom of your keyboard\.
