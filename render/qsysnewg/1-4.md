## 1.4 Output Devices

<a id="FIGUNIQ4"></a>

![Resource 4](4.png)

PICTURE 4.

Printers: One of the most common output devices. Printed output can be in many forms, from a complex report of hundreds of pages to a sales receipt from the supermarket checkout counter. Computer-produced speech: In the United States, when you call for directory assistance, the computer responds to your push button telephone by using computer-produced speech. You're probably using one right now: your **display** **station**. Input devices, output devices, and devices that combine both input and output are sometimes referred to as **work** **stations**.
