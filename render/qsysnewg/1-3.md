The scanner recognizes the pattern of lines in the product code and transfers the information to a computer. Magnetic card reader: Used at banks in automated teller machines. The card reader reads the details of your account from the magnetic pattern on the bank card. Push button telephone: Used to provide services from your own home. The computer recognizes the tone associated with the button you push.
