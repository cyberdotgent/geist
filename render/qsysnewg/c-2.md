## C.2 Changing Your Message Delivery Mode

ST| Changing Your Message Delivery Mode | You can specify your message delivery mode by changing your message queue as follows:

1. Type **CHGMSGQ** (Change Message Queue) on any command line. 2. Press F4 (Prompt); the Change Message Queue display is shown. Change Message Queue (CHGMSGQ) Type choices, press Enter. Message queue . . . . . . . . . _____________ Name, *USRPRF, *WRKSTN Library . . . . . . . . . . . *LIBL Name, *LIBL, *CURLIB all | Delivery . . . . . . . . . . . . *SAME__ *SAME, *HOLD, *BREAK... | Severity code filter . . . . . . *SAME_ 0-99, *SAME | Text 'description' . . . . . . . *SAME______________________________________ | ______ Bottom F3=Exit F4=Prompt F5=Refresh F10=Additional parameters F12=Cancel F13=How to use this display F24=More keys 3. Type the name of your message queue (usually the same as your user ID). 4. Type the delivery mode you want to use (Break, Notify, Hold, Default). (The three dots after *BREAK in the display above mean that there are more values than can be shown on that line. Put your cursor on the line and press the Help key to see them all.) 5. Press the Enter key; your message queue is changed.
