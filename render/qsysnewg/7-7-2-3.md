#### 7.7.2.3 Break Messages

| When you receive break messages that don't need a reply, simply press the      | Enter key after carefully reading the message to get back to the display ?  | you were working on before the interruption.      | If the break message needs a reply, just type the reply on the line        | provided and press the Enter key.  The display you were working on before     | the interruption then reappears, and you can continue your work.

**Note:** Remember, occasionally the last few characters entered before an interruption may be lost.
