## 3\.2 Keyboard Summary

- **Function keys** have special and powerful functions\. There are two kinds:

Engraved \(have words, abbreviations, or symbols on them\) Numbered \(up to 24\)

- There are many keyboards that can be used on the AS/400 system\. You can find information about them in [Appendix E, "AS/400 Keyboards\."](e-0.md)
- **Active** function keys are shown on the bottom of displays\.
- The most commonly used F keys are:

F1=Help F3=Exit F4=Prompt F5=Refresh F12=Cancel F24=More keys
