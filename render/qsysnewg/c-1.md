## C.1 Changing Your Initial Menu

| and is noted in your user profile. However, you may want another menu to | be your initial menu whenever you sign on. The easiest way to change the | initial menu is by using the F23 key. To do this, go to the menu you want | and press F23. The next time you sign on, that menu will be your initial menu. Want to try it OK. | Let's say that the AS/400 Main Menu is currently your initial menu, but | your job requires you to go to the Files, Libraries, and Folders menu almost all of the time. There is no need to waste time looking at the Main Menu each time, so you want to make the Files, Libraries, and Folders menu your initial menu. Do the following: | 1. Go to the Files, Libraries, and Folders menu (using the Go command, | you would simply type `go` `data` on a command line). 2. Press F23.

**Wait** **a** **Minute!**

There is no F23 key listed on the bottom! OK, we fibbed a little. We've said several times that the keys listed on the bottom of the menus were the active **F key**s. That's true, but there are really more active F keys than are listed on the display. Now that you're becoming an expert, you can handle the remaining steps. | You can use the F23 key from most non-Operational Assistant menus. | You should note that depending upon how your system applications | have been set up, they could possibly override any initial menu | change that you've made using the F23 key. 3. A message appears: `User` `Profile` `(your` `user` `name)` `Changed`. 4. You probably want to change your initial menu back to the AS/400 Main Menu. Press F16; press F23. That's it.
