### 4\.1\.3 Advanced Assistance Level

This assistance level provides displays that offer the same functions and options as the intermediate assistance level\. However, to increase the amount of work space on the display, available options and functions are not actually shown on the display\.

**Note:** The advanced assistance level is only available on a few of the system displays\.

This guide provides examples of how to perform essential AS/400 tasks using the basic assistance level, or Operational Assistant, as it is otherwise referred to\.
