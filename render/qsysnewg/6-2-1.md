### 6.2.1 Using Hypertext

You can quickly navigate the large amount of help information provided on

the AS/400 system through the use of **hypertext**. Hypertext is a series of AS/400 help information displays that are linked together by key words or phrases. As you were looking through the various help displays, did you notice that some of the words or phrases were specially highlighted The highlighting could either be yellow and underlined on color displays, or bright and underlined on monochrome displays. Such words or phrases are called

**reference** **phrases**. A reference phrase indicates that a **hypertext** **link** to additional help information exists. As you browse a help display, you may find a reference phrase that is closely related to the information you're looking for. To get to an information display that provides more specific information about that word or phrase, use the Field Advance key to move the cursor in front of the reference phrase, and press the Enter key. A display of information specific to that word or phrase appears.

Notice that the topics you have already chosen have the **>** symbol in front of them. This symbol indicates that you've already looked at them, which helps to narrow your search. There is another method of backtracking to a previous hypertext topic display. Pressing the F12 key (Cancel) takes you back a single hypertext topic display on the path. This process may be repeated any number of times until you arrive at the original help display from which hypertext was entered.
