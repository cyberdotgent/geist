### 1.5.3 Diskettes

<a id="FIGUNIQ7"></a>

![Resource 7](resource:7)

PICTURE 7.

Diskettes work on a principle that is similar to magnetic disks, but they are slower and hold less data. The device that reads and writes to diskettes is called a diskette drive. Like magnetic disks, diskettes offer permanent storage. Unlike most magnetic disks, diskettes can be removed from the diskette drives. Diskettes can be easily stored and transported, even sent by mail. This means they can be used for transferring data from one system to another. Diskettes are sometimes called **floppy** **disks**. They are most well known for their use with personal computers, although they are used with computers of all sizes.
