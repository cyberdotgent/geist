## 2\.4 Your First Menu

What you are looking at now is a **menu**\. *Which* menu you see depends on how your administrator has set up your user profile\. User profiles are discussed in ["User Profiles and Security Levels" in topic 4\.3](4-3.md)\.

**Note:** The menus shown in this book may have more options than those shown on your display station\. The exact appearance of the menus depends on your **user class**, which needn't concern us right now\.

The AS/400 system provides two major menus from which you can perform a variety of system tasks: the AS/400 Main Menu and the AS/400 Operational Assistant menu\.

The AS/400 Main Menu accesses the more complex AS/400 functions\. It also enables the use of most of the functions available on the AS/400 system\.

The AS/400 Operational Assistant menu provides easy access to the most commonly performed AS/400 functions\. This guide presents the basic AS/400 tasks that can be performed through the use of the AS/400 Operational Assistant menu\.
