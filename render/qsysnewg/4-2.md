## 4.2 Changing Your Assistance Level

| current display: | 1. Press F21 (Select assistance level). The Select Assistance Level | window appears on the current display. | **Not**e: If F21 (Select assistance level) isn't available on your | current display, then there are no other assistance levels | available for that display. | Work with Jobs | ................................................................ m: RCH38360 | Select Assistance Level | | Current assistance level . Basic | | | Type choice below, then press Enter. | | Assistance level . . . . . 1 1=Basic | 2=Intermediate | | | | F1=Help F12=Cancel | | :..............................................................: | Bottom an | F1=Help F3=Exit F5=Refresh F9=Command line F11=Display dates/times | F12=Cancel F14=Select other jobs F21=Select assistance level | 2. This window indicates what your current assistance level is and what | assistance levels are available for the current display. | 3. Type `a` 1 to select the basic assistance level, `a` 2 to select the | intermediate assistance level, or `a` 3 to select the advanced | assistance level (if available). Then press the Enter key. | 4. The version of the display that corresponds to the assistance level | you've chosen now appears. From now on, this is the version of the | display that will appear (unless you should change assistance levels | again).

|

**Stuck** **in** **Another** **Assistance** **Level?** | Don't panic! It's a simple matter to change back to your previous | level. If you're not at a display that allows you to use the F21 key, | just press the F3 (Exit) key repeatedly until you get back to the | display that does allow it. Then press the F21 (Select assistance | level) key, and choose the assistance level you want.
