### 7.4.1 AS/400 System Messages

system:

`Press` `Ready` `or` `Start` `on` `device` `PRT01.` The computer is telling you that your printer needs to be turned on before

you can get your print job going. | If you put the cursor on a system message and press the Help key, you see | the Additional Message Information display. On this display, the message is repeated and additional information is included. This additional | information can be quite helpful. For instance, it can explain how to | recover from an error.
