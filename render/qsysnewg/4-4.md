## 4.4 Assistance Level and User Profile Summary

| Your **assistance** **level** determines the amount of involvement that the | computer has in performing your tasks, as well as the complexity of | the displays you use. There are three assistance levels available on | the AS/400 system: | - Basic (system provides the most assistance) | - Intermediate | - Advanced (system provides the least assistance) | **Note:** The advanced assistance level is only available on a few of the | system displays. | The tasks in this guide are presented using the basic assistance level | (also referred to as the AS/400 Operational Assistant). | You can change the assistance level from many AS/400 displays by | pressing the F21 key. | Your **user** **profile** and the system **security** **level** determine both the | amount and the complexity of the information that you see displayed.
