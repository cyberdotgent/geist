## B.2 Using the Sign on Display

| Welcome back! | You should have the Sign On display in front of you now. (If you never | went anywhere, you'll have to pretend you did, sign off, and get the Sign | On display back on). Now, let's say that you want to go directly from the | Sign On display to the Documentation and Problem Handling menu.

Type your *User* *name* and your *Password* as you usually do. Then, move your

cursor down two fields to the *Menu* line. Type in the menu ID, **userhelp**. Press the Enter key, and the Documentation and Problem Handling menu appears!
