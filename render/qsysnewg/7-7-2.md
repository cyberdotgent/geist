### 7.7.2 Getting an Inquiry Message

The format of inquiry messages that you get in your message queue depends on whether they were sent by the system or a person.

| *All* inquiry messages, including those that interrupt a user, provide a | reply line after the message. You answer the message on this line and send it back to its source-person, program, or machine.
