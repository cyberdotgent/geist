### 7.5.1 Message Line

communicates with you while you are working. The messages you receive on the message line tell you about job or system status, user errors, or responses that you make. At the bottom of the following Work with Jobs display is an example of a system message on the message line: Work with Jobs System: RCH38360 User . . . . . NORBERT Type options below, then press Enter. / 3=Hold 4=Delete (End) 6=Release 7=Display message 8=Work with printer output Job Queue/ Opt Job Status QCTL | _ QJSCCPY Running | QXFPCS | _ QXFSERV Running | Bottom all | F1=Help F3=Exit F5=Refresh F9=Command line F11=Display dates/times | F12=Cancel F14=Select other jobs F21=Select assistance level

**Already** **at** **bottom** **of** **area.**

If you didn't notice the word `Bottom` in the lower right corner of the display and pressed the Page Down key, the computer would immediately remind you with a message like this:

| `Already` `at` `bottom` `of` `area.`

Message line help: You can find out what caused the message, or how you

| may need to respond to it, by moving your cursor down to the message line and pressing the Help key.
