## 7.1 Informational Messages

Informational messages don't require a reply. For example, last night, before you left work, you sent a big job to the printer. This morning when you signed on to the system, you were notified that your job had been printed:

`J`o`b Invento`r`y Li`s`t Complete`d. This message does not require a response. It's an informational message. Here is another example: You are entering data on newly hired employees in your company. While you're working, a message flashes on your display:

`System` `Being` `Shut` `Down` `at` `9:00.` This message doesn't require a response from you. It's an informational message sent by the system operator.
