## 1\.9 Summary

- A **Typical Computer System** can be used in many places:

In an office In a warehouse In a laboratory In a doughnut factory

- All computers have three main functions:

Input Process Output

- A fourth function of computers is **storage**\.
- The operation of these functions is controlled by a **program**\.
- The most important program is the **operating system**\.
- Computer hardware is composed of:

Input devices Output devices Storage devices The processor

- The **AS/400 System** is a powerful computer with three types of system units:

9402 9404 9406

- The operating system is **OS/400**\.
- Norbert will help you learn how to control the computer\.
