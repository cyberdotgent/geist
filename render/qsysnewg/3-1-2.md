### 3.1.2 Numbered Function Keys

Keyboards attached to your display station or personal computer can have from 10 to 24 of these numbered function keys. On most keyboards, including personal computers, the function keys are the

top row of keys or are in a **pad** on the left side of the keyboard. They

have **F**s on them, so you'll probably start calling them **F** **keys** instead of function keys.

**This** **Is** **Important**

| On other keyboards, to use the numbered function keys, you use a Cmd or Alt key together with the number keys at the top of the keyboard (see Ap [pendix E, "AS/400 Keyboards" f](e-0.md) or the functions available on | your keyboard).
