#### 5\.10\.9\.6 What Happens to Your VM/ESA Communication Partner

When you invoke RTRVBFR, all your non\-control APPC/VM and IUCV communication paths are severed\. APPC/VM informs your communication partners as if you issued a SEVER TYPE=ABEND\. See page [5\.10\.12\.9](5-10-12-9.md) for a description of possible sever codes\.
