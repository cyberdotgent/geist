#### 2.3.12.1 Gaining Control

Your external interrupt handler must gain control whenever your virtual machine receives an external interrupt. Again, the specifics of interrupt handling vary from operating system to operating system. However, you

should make sure that your interrupt handler disables external interrupts until current processing completes and enables them before returning control.
