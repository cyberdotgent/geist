## 2.4.18 TESTMSG (Test Message)

<a id="HDRTESTMSG"></a>

Use the TESTMSG function to determine whether you have any messages

pending on your send queue or replies pending on your reply queue. **Notes:** 1. When you receive messages or replies, IUCV automatically informs you by reflecting a message-pending or message-complete external interrupt to your virtual machine. Therefore, unless you disable your virtual machine for IUCV message-pending and message-complete external interrupts, do not use the TESTMSG function. 2. If, when your virtual machine invokes the TESTMSG function, it finds that there are no messages or replies pending, your virtual machine enters a wait state. 3. If, while your virtual machine is in a wait state, you receive an IUCV message or reply, your virtual machine resumes execution by reexecuting the TESTMSG function (which returns a condition code).
