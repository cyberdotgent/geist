#### 3.8.5.9 What Happens to Your VM Communication Partner

You can only issue SENDCNFD in response to a SENDCNF for SYNCLVL=CONFIRM or SYNCLVL=SYNCPT conversations. SENDCNFD always causes the completion of the SENDCNF. For SYNCLVL=SYNCPT conversations, SENDCNFD can also be issued in response to your partner's backout sync-point. If your communication partner issued SENDCNF with WAIT=NO, and your partner is enabled for function complete interrupts, then your partner gets a

function complete interrupt.
