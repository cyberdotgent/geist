## 3.8.4.8 SENDCNF Completion

<a id="HDRCNFCMP"></a>

#### SENDCNF Completion

After issuing a SENDCNF, you cannot issue another SEND [(16)](#FTNSENDFN), RECEIVE, or SEVER TYPE=NORMAL on that path until the outstanding SENDCNF is complete. SENDCNF is complete when the communication partner responds with a SENDCNFD, SENDERR, or SEVER.
