### 7\.12\.1\.7 HCPRWA

<a id="HDRDMKRWA"></a>

CP's version of HCPRWA contains an entry point, HCPRWACP, which is empty, non\-executable, static, and resident\.

An ESM may replace CP's stub module with its own copy of HCPRWA\. The ESM's HCPRWA can serve as a work area for the other ACI\-exit modules\.

**PSPI end**
