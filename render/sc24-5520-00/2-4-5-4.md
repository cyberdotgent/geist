#### 2\.4\.5\.4 PURGE Parameter List Format

The format of the PURGE parameter list is:

<a id="FIGFIGUNIQ61"></a>

```
      IPARML DSECT

      0      1        2         3         4         5         6       7
      ______________ _________ __________ ________________________________ 
    0|   IPPATHID   | IPFLAGS1| IPRCODE  |                 IPMSGID        |
     |______________|_________|__________|________________________________|
    8|   IPAUDIT    |   ///////////////////////////////////////////////// |
     |______________|____________________ ________________________________|
   10| ///////////////////////////////   |            IPSRCCLS            |
     |___________________________________|________________________________|
   18|           IPMSGTAG                | ////////////////////////////// |
     |___________________________________|________________________________|
   20| ////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// |
     |____________________________________________________________________|
```
