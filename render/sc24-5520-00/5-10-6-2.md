#### 5\.10\.6\.2 Required Parameters

`PRMLIST=` specifies the address of the IUCV DCLBFR parameter list\. The address must be a guest real address, that is, the address must be within the virtual machine's real address space \(guest=real\)\. Also, the parameter list must be on a doubleword boundary\.

*label* is the relocatable label of the parameter list\.

`(`*reg*`)` is the register number that contains the address of the parameter list\.
