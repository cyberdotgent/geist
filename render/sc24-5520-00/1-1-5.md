## 1\.1\.5 How Addresses Are Processed

<a id="HDRADPROC"></a>

VM/ESA processes addresses according to the mode in which a guest is running\.

<a id="TBLTBLUNIQ1"></a>

```
    ____________________________________ ____________________________________
   | Type/Mode of Guest:                | Address Length:                    |
   |____________________________________|____________________________________|
   | System/370, BC mode                | 24-bit                             |
   |____________________________________|____________________________________|
   | System/370, EC mode                | 31-bit                             |
   |____________________________________|____________________________________|
   | ESA/370 and ESA/390                | 24- or 31-bit, depending on the    |
   |                                    | addressing mode (1)                |
   |____________________________________|____________________________________|
```

Due to the asynchronous function of IUCV, parameter lists are to be treated as guest real addresses, while all other data areas are to be treated as above\.

Unless otherwise noted, all addresses processed by VM DIAGNOSE codes or macros are guest real addresses\. For an explanation of "real" and "absolute" addressing, see the publication, *IBM Enterprise Systems Architecture/370 Principles of Operation*\.

<a id="FTNONE"></a>

\(1\) Exception: I/O DIAGNOSE and IUCV codes are always processed in 31\-bit mode\.
