## 2.3.12.4 Returning Control

<a id="HDRS4"></a>

#### Returning Control

When your program has processed the IUCV interrupt, return control to the program that was running when your external interrupt handler gained control. How you do this depends upon how you coded your external interrupt handler to gain control.
