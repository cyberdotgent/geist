#### 5.10.10.3 Optional Parameters

MF=L expands the IUCV macro to generate the instructions necessary to initialize the parameter list as specified, but not to invoke the SETCMASK function. MASK= specifies the mask byte to determine for which, if any, of the APPC/VM and IUCV external interrupts a virtual machine is enabled for. label is the relocatable label of a byte containing the mask. (reg) is the register number that contains the mask in its low-order byte.
