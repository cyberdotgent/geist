**Note:** When a virtual machine sends you a message, IUCV automatically informs you of the message by reflecting a message-pending external interrupt to your virtual machine. Therefore, unless you disable your virtual machine for IUCV message-pending external interrupts, do not use the DESCRIBE function. TESTCMPL. Therefore, unless you disable your virtual machine for IUCV message-complete external interrupts, do not use the TESTCMPL function. TESTMSG.
