#### 2\.4\.6\.1 QUERY Outputs

When you invoke the QUERY function, IUCV returns:

- The size of the external interrupt buffer that IUCV requires in general register 0\.
- The maximum number of connections that your virtual machine can have outstanding in general register 1\.
