#### 2.4.17.1 TESTCMPL Required Input

*The* *address* *of* *the* *TESTCMPL* *parameter* *list*. Use the PRMLIST=parameter to identify to IUCV the address of your parameter list. The address of the parameter list must be a guest real address (an address that is real to your virtual machine), and it must be on a doubleword boundary. Specify the address of the parameter list as (a) a relocatable label, or (b) the number of a register that contains the address.
