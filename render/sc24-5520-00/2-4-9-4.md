#### 2\.4\.9\.4 REJECT Parameter List Format

The format of the REJECT parameter list is:

<a id="FIGFIGUNIQ69"></a>

```
      IPARML DSECT

      0      1        2         3         4         5         6       7
      ______________ _________ _________ _________________________________ 
    0|   IPPATHID   | IPFLAGS1| IPRCODE |                 IPMSGID         |
     |______________|_________|_________|_________________________________|
    8|          IPTRGCLS                | /////////////////////////////// |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   10| ////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// |
     |____________________________________________________________________|
   18| ////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// |
     |____________________________________________________________________|
   20| ////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// |
     |____________________________________________________________________|
```
