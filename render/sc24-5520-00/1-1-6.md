## 1.1.6 Condition Codes, Return Codes, and Exceptions

<a id="HDRRESP"></a>

Once CP executes a DIAGNOSE code, certain responses may be sent back to you in order to indicate whether or not the execution was successful. Three types of responses that you may receive are: Cond**ition cod**e**s (so**metimes referred to as comp**letion cod**e**s), w**hich appear in the PSW (program status word). Retu**rn cod**e**s, wh**ich appear in a register. The exact register where they appear is specified in the "Responses" section of the documentation for each DIAGNOSE code. Exce**ptions, wh**ich are program interruptions. The "Responses" section of each DIAGNOSE code explains the significance of all applicable condition codes and return codes.
