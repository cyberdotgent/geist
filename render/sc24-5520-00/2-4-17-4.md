#### 2\.4\.17\.4 TESTCMPL Parameter List Format

The format of the TESTCMPL parameter list is:

<a id="FIGFIGUNIQ85"></a>

```
      IPARML DSECT

      0      1        2         3         4         5         6       7
      ______________ _________ _________ _________________________________ 
    0|   IPPATHID   | IPFLAGS1| IPRCODE |                 IPMSGID         |
     |______________|_________|_________|_________________________________|
    8|   IPAUDIT    | ///////////////// |            IPRMMSG1             |
     |______________|___________________|_________________________________|
   10|          IPRMMSG2                |            IPSRCCLS             |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   18|          IPMSGTAG                |//////////////////////////////// |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   20|          IPBFLN2F                |//////////////////////////////// |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
```
