### 8\.13\.2\.1 Section 1 \(Environmental Data\)

Section 1 contains the record header with basic environmental data\. The application initializes this area to binary zeros and stores the characters "SR" in the record header\. The environmental data of section 1 is filled in automatically when DIAGNOSE code X'94' SR processing is invoked\. The environmental data that is stored in this section provides a system context within which the software errors can be viewed\. Section 1 includes items such as:

- The CPU model and serial number
- The date and time, with a time zone conversion factor
- The customer\-assigned system name
- The product ID of the control program\.
