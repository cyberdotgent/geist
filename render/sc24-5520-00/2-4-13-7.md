#### 2\.4\.13\.7 SEND Parameter List Format

The format of the SEND parameter list is:

<a id="FIGFIGUNIQ77"></a>

```
      IPARML DSECT

      0      1        2         3         4         5         6       7
      ______________ _________ _________ _________________________________ 
    0|   IPPATHID   | IPFLAGS1| IPRCODE |                   IPMSGID       |
     |______________|_________|_________|_________________________________|
    8|           IPTRGCLS               |          IPBFADR1/IPRMMSG1      |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   10|       IPBFLN1F/IPRMMSG2          |              IPSRCCLS           |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   18|           IPMSGTAG               |              IPBFADR2           |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   20|           IPBFLN2F               | /////////////////////////////// |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
```
