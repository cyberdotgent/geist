## 3.8.8.9 What Happens to Your VM Communication Partner

<a id="HDRSRWHAT"></a>

#### What Happens to Your VM Communication Partner

If your communication partner is enabled for SENDREQ interrupts, it gets [FIGSRQRSI](#FIGSRQRSI)

the SENDREQ interrupt shown in Figure 52.

<a id="SRQRSI IPARML DSECT 0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 0 IPPATHID ////// IPTYPE ///////////////////////////////// 8 ///////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// 10 ///////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// 18 ///////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// 20 ///////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// Figure 52. SENDREQ (Request-to-Send) Interrupt"></a>

**IPPATHID** is the path ID on which you get the SENDREQ notice. **IPTYPE** is the interrupt type for a SENDREQ notification (IPTYPSRA, X'88'). CP does not queue more than one SENDREQ interrupt on a single path for the communication partner at one time. Thus, the number of SENDREQ interrupts reflected to your communication partner may be less than the number of SENDREQs issued. You cannot receive SENDREQ indicators with the RECEIVE function. They are only presented as an interrupt or with the DESCRIBE function.
