## 3.8.5 APPCVM SENDCNFD (Send Confirmed)

<a id="HDRAPSCNFD"></a>

Use the SENDCNFD (Send Confirmed) function to send a confirmation response from your program to another program. You should issue this as a positive response to your partner sending a SENDCNF (or to confirm your partner's backout sync-point). (For a negative response, invoke SENDERR; see

["APPCVM SENDERR (Send Error)" in topic 3.8.7](3-8-7.md).)
