#### 2\.4\.10\.5 REPLY Parameter List Format

The format of the REPLY parameter list is:

<a id="FIGFIGUNIQ72"></a>

```
      IPARML DSECT

      0      1        2         3         4         5         6       7
      ______________ _________ _________ _________________________________ 
    0|   IPPATHID   | IPFLAGS1| IPRCODE |                  IPMSGID        |
     |______________|_________|_________|_________________________________|
    8|             IPTRGCLS             |            IPRMMSG1             |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   10|             IPRMMSG2             |///////////////////////////      |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   18| //////////////////////////////   |            IPBFADR2             |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
   20|             IPBFLN2F             | /////////////////////////       |
     |__________________________________|_________________________________|
```
