#### 2.4.3.7 Program Exceptions

The following program exceptions can occur if you invoke the DCLBFR function incorrectly: Specification. You did not define your parameter list on a doubleword boundary. Addressing--one of the following: Your parameter list address is invalid; the address you specified is outside of your virtual machine's storage. You specified an invalid buffer address. Operation. Your virtual machine was not in supervisor state.
