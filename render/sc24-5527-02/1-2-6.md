### 1.2.6 Placing the Serviced Components Into Production

| Once you have tested the new service and are satisfied with the results | for all serviced components, you should put them into production. You need to: Save your Named Saved System (NSS) and Discontiguous Saved Segments (DCSS) Place the test disks into production Put CMS into production Bring your new CP online if it was tested second-level Merge your tested service to your production service disks.
