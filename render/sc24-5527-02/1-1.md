## 1.1 Overview of the Product Service Upgrade (PSU) Procedure

The Product Service Upgrade (PSU) is a procedure that refreshes the service database disks defined for VM/ESA Release 2, leaves customized files untouched, and preserves any reach-ahead service. This procedure

utilizes the current VM/ESA Recommended Service Upgrade (RSU), which has all service preinstalled.

The PSU procedure cannot be used to migrate from one release to another, but is intended to upgrade your current VM/ESA Release 2 service via the RSU tape. Because service from the RSU tape is preapplied and objects are already built, much time can be saved. The saved segments must be rebuilt since they cannot be taken from the tapes. The PSU procedure is the method of installing preventive maintenance to | VM/ESA Release 2.
