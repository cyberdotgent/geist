## 3.14 Step 12.  Placing Your Serviced Components Into Production

<a id="HDRPOST"></a>

This step assumes that you have tested the service that you have received and are ready to put it into production. It also assumes that you have serviced all components for this service session. In this procedure you will: Rebuild the saved segments Place the test build disks into production Rebuild the CMS saved system Place the CP nucleus into production Place TSAF and AVS into production Place GCS into production, and Merge your tested service to your production service disks.
