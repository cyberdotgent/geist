## D\.2 Building a Nucleus Outside of the Service Process

If you need to build a nucleus outside of the service process see [Chapter 4, "Detailed Component Build Steps" in topic 4\.0](4-0.md) for the component being processed and locate the task for rebuilding the nucleus\. The "Rebuild the Nucleus" step contains the procedure that must be followed when building a nucleus for the component\.
