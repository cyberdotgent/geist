## 2\.1 Materials Needed for a Product Service Upgrade

<a id="HDRBOOKS"></a>

- Obtain the latest VM/ESA RSU Tape:

You need to order the VM/ESA RSU Tape\. A Program Reorder Form will have been sent to you to obtain the RSU Tape or you can reorder the RSU Tape through the IBM Support Center\.

- In addition to this book, you will also need the following documentation:

[VM/ESA: Installation Guide](<DOCNUM/SC24-5526/CCONTENTS>), SC24\-5431

[VM/ESA: VMSES/E Introduction and Reference](<DOCNUM/SC24-5444/CCONTENTS>), SC24\-5444

Program Directory\.

- Also, contact your support center for the latest installation information, for example, the RSU Tape bucket\.
