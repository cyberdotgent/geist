## PREFACE.3 What This Book Contains

This book contains the Product Service Upgrade (PSU) service procedure, and the corrective (COR) service procedure for all VM/ESA components. Also, it contains information on using VMFSIM to manage your Software Inventories while servicing VM. Local service, and removing service are also discussed.
