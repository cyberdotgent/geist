## 3.12 Step 10.  Service TSAF

<a id="HDRPCTSF"></a>

Note If you do not have TSAF service on the tape, go to ["Step 11.](3-13.md) [Service AVS" in topic 3.13](3-13.md). Loading TSAF to either an SFS directory or to a CMS minidisk was an installation step option made during the VM/ESA installation process.
