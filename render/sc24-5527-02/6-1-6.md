#### 6.1.6 Determine APARs Contained in PTFs

To determine what APARs are contained in all PTFs currently received the following VMFSIM QUERY command would be issued against the

table. The

table contains a list of all APARs contained in PTFs.

<a id="TBLTBLUNIQ438"></a>

| VMFSIP2480I RESULTS FOR | TDATA :APARNUM | :PTF UM18082 |
| --- | --- | --- |


**Field** **Data** **Definition** **:****PTF** This field contains the PTF number. **:****APARNUM** This field contains the list of APARs contained in the PTF field.
