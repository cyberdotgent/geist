## 7\.2 Step 1\. Prepare for Local Service

<a id="HDRLOCPREP"></a>

**1\.** If you have not already prepared your system to apply new service, complete ["Step 3\. Prepare the System for Service" in topic 3\.5](3-5.md) before continuing\.

**2\.** Access the necessary disks:

```
 |                                                esalcl is the local service $PPF.  If you
 | access 51d d/d                                 have an override to esalcl, use it instead.

 |                                                identifier, unless you use your own
 |                                                override file.  The default component
```
