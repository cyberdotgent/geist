## 8\.1 Removing Service Levels

<a id="HDRRMVLVL"></a>

The following service levels are supported:

- The alternate \(current\) service level
- The test \(intermediate\) service level
- The production service level\.

Each service level is located on a separate disk\. For example, the access order specified in the default PPF for CP is:

**APPLY \(2A6\)** Alternate apply disk

**APPLY \(2A4\)** Intermediate apply disk

**APPLY \(2A2\)** Production apply disk

You can remove \(back out\) service levels by removing the appropriate disks from the access order defined in the PPF and rebuilding all objects that were affected by the level being removed\. The following section identifies the steps required to remove the current service level and all service back to the production service level\.
