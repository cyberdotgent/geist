## PREFACE\.1 Who Should Read This Document

This document is intended primarily for IBM technical professionals and customer personnel who require an overview of Client/Server computing and need to know what VM/ESA is doing to position itself in this technology\.

Readers include IBM and customer technical professionals and, in general, VM systems specialists, systems programmers, and systems planners\.
