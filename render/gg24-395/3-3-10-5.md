### 3\.3\.10\.5 New Features in LFS/ESA 1\.1\.2

LFS/ESA 1\.1\.2 for VM adds new administrator and end\-user commands in the NFS environment and enhanced support for UNIX\-style export files\. The new administrator commands are:

- LIST \- Displays the contents and attributes of a LFS/ESA directory
- ERASE \- Erases one or more files from a LFS/ESA directory

The new end\-user commands are:

- showexport
- showmount

As well, IBM has announced its intention to provide LFS/ESA as a feature of VM/ESA V2 and intends to rename LFS/ESA to position it within the family of IBM LAN Server products\.
