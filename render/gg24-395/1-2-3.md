## 1\.2\.3 Is This Open

As one can read everywhere, "open" means many things to many people, and its definition varies in time\.

SAA is a vital player in open standards, with many parts of SAA being well accepted in the industry\. But with the huge growth in UNIX applications, customers needed something more than SAA\.

To provide customers with that function, OpenEdition VM and MVS will become the strategic direction for providing synergy between the SAA and UNIX platforms\. But why do customers need or want this synergy? One answer is that customers are looking for ways of building their systems that are based on standards, rather than on vendor products\. Dan Tapscott, president of New Paradigm Learning Corporation puts it this way:

"Leading organizations everywhere are embracing the concept of a standards\-based architecture\. Such architectures are modular, flexible, enterprise\-wide, based on standards rather than specific vendor products, and owned by the customer, not the suppliers\. As such, they enable the IT \(Information Technology\) infrastructure required for competitiveness and success in the new business environment\."

This "new business environment" is the driver for the concept of open computing\. Let's take a closer look at what open is, why it is needed and IBM's direction in this environment\.
