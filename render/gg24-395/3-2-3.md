<a id="HDRHGUI100"></a>

<a id="TBLTBLUNIQ7"></a>

![PICTURE 33](33.png)

```
 |

 |                 When comparing the look of a 3270 style application with a
 |                 modern, mouse controlled, graphical workstation application,
 |                 there is no doubt that the 3270 screen isn't fancy any longer.
 |                 On the other hand, many 3270 applications still do a good job
 |                 and up to now, most mission critical business applications are
 |                 3270-based.

 |                 When more end users acquire powerful workstations, a business
 |                 may want to modernize these mainframe applications, while
 |                 keeping them on the host.  This will not only make the
 |                 application look better, but can also improve end-user
 |                 productivity by easing data entry and eliminating the investment
 |                 of learning a new interface.  VM is no exception to that: more
 |                 than half of VM's end users have graphical workstations.
```
