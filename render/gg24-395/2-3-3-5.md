### 2\.3\.3\.5 Systems Management Services

<a id="HDRHDSS105"></a>

The Open Blueprint's Systems Management is based on the SystemView structure \(this is discussed in more detail in Section ["SystemView" in](3-3-6.md) [topic 3\.3\.6](3-3-6.md)\)\. It includes business, change, configuration, operations, performance and problem management\. Note that in [Figure 11 in topic 2\.3\.3](11.png) systems management is shown horizontally across the top to illustrate that it covers all of the elements within the Open Blueprint\.

In the Open Blueprint, Systems Management supports the following standards:

- OSI X\.700 Management Model
- Simple Network Management Protocol \(SNMP\)
- Common Management Information Protocol \(CMIP\)
- Systems Network Architecture/Management Services \(SNA/MS\)
- X/OPEN Management Protocol \(XMP\)
- Emerging standards POSIX System Administration Services \(1003\.7\)
- The Desktop Management Task Force \(DTMF\) Desktop Management Interface \(DMI\)
