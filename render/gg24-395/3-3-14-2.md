### 3\.3\.14\.2 Sync Point Processing

<a id="HDRHCRR102"></a>

When an application issues a commit, the SPM starts sync point processing, also known as the CRR coordination function\. During sync point processing, the SPM calls five exit routines that have to be provided by the RA\. These exits are:

- Precoordination
- Coordination
- Postcoordination
- End of logical unit of work
- Rollback

The precoordination and coordination exits are used to ask the resource manager if it is ready to commit and then to perform the commit, respectively\. This is the implementation of a 2\-phase commit protocol\. The flow of a 2\-phase commit is as follows:

1\. Phase One

a\. **Prepare:**

The SPM asks each of the protected resources and protected conversations if they are ready to commit\.

b\. **Request commit:**

The protected resources and protected conversations reply that either they can or cannot commit\. If one cannot commit, then the SPM performs a rollback and tells all other protected resources and protected conversations to do the same\. If they can all commit, then phase two begins\.

2\. Phase Two

a\. **Committed:**

The SPM tells the protected resources and protected conversations to perform the commit\.

b\. **Forget:**

The protected resources and protected conversations reply to the SPM that the commit has been done successfully and the CRR log for this logical unit of work can now be erased\.
