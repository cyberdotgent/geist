## 1\.1\.2 Client/Server Expectations and Requirements

<a id="HDRHWIC300"></a>

Whatever the forces that create change are, when a solution is proposed, even only in name, people create their own view of what that solution entails\.

Client/Server is a term that has not had a strong definition, and many interpretations of what it really means have been proposed\. It is worthwhile therefore, in gaining an understanding of what Client/Server means, to understand some of the expectations that businesses have of it\.
