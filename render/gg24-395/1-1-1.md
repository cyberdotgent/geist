## 1\.1\.1 Definitions

<a id="HDRHWIC200"></a>

There are several concepts often recognized as being close to, but not the same as the words "Client/Server\." Often terms such as cooperative processing and distributed computing are used interchangeably\. In actuality there is an overlap in what the terms mean\. In this section, we will explain what we think the distinction is\.
