## PREFACE\.2 What's New in This Edition

The introduction of DCE, POSIX and the CMS GUI in VM/ESA Version 2 will open VM/ESA to exciting new possibilities in distributed computing\. This document has been updated to describe these new functions and their relationship to Client/Server computing\. As well, we have also updated the existing material to reflect the latest industry directions with respect to an open, distributed computing environment\.

Where the addition of new or enhanced functions or rewording for improved user understanding caused changes to the text and illustrations, modifications are flagged with a vertical bar on the left, as in this paragraph\. Minor editorial corrections are not flagged\.
