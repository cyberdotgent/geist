### 3\.3\.16\.1 Third Generation Languages

As mentioned earlier, the Open Blueprint model shows an environment where language choice is based on local system criteria\. Other software enables the connectivity aspects of the application\.

Language products that currently are available for the VM/ESA platform are:

- Ada/370 \- 5706\-292
- SAA AD/Cycle C/370 \- 5688\-040
- SAA AD/Cycle COBOL/370 \- 5688\-197
- VS COBOL II \- 5740\-CB1
- VS FORTRAN \- 5668\-087
- VS Pascal \- 5668\-767
- REXX in VM/ESA \- 5684\-112
- SAA REXX/370 \(Rexx compiler\) \- 5695\-013
- SAA AD/Cycle PLI \- 5688\-235
- OS PL/I \- 5668\-910

The VM/ESA implementations of these languages conform to recognized standards as listed in [Appendix A, "Additional Standards and Standards](appendix1-1.md) [Organizations" in topic APPENDIX1\.1](appendix1-1.md)\.
