### 2\.2\.1\.6 Architecture\-Neutral Distribution Format

<a id="HDRHOSF103"></a>

Architecture\-Neutral Distribution Format \(ANDF\) is a compiler intermediate language technology that enables developers to develop and distribute their applications in a format that can be installed and run on diverse Open Systems architectures\. This technology, designed in a hardware\-independent software format, provides a consistent development and distribution environment for multiple platforms\.

Thus, ANDF facilitates the creation of highly portable, high performance applications for the Open Systems marketplace\. This product is not available for licensing at the moment\.
