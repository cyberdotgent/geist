## 2\.1\.3 The Connection between Client/Server and Open

<a id="HDRHDEF100"></a>

It is sometimes hard to distinguish between the terms *Client/Server* and *Open*\. Client/Server implementations often consist of hardware and software from many different vendors\. With so many differing standards and protocols, many Client/Server configurations are difficult, if not impossible, to implement\. Open standards enable these systems to interact more easily, thus reducing complexity and greatly increasing the chances of success\.

If you compare the technical requirements of Client/Server \(in Section ["Technical Requirements" in topic 1\.1\.2\.2](1-1-2-2.md)\) with the open infrastructure requirements covered in this chapter, you will see how Client/Server and Open Systems are intertwined\.

If an enterprise maintains a systems base involving only systems that conform to open standards, then they should have the ability to fully exploit Client/Server concepts\. Application components could be split at boundaries that make best use of the facilities available, and not at arbitrary lines imposed by vendors\.

The following chapters will discuss some computing models that have been proposed, along with products that are available to enable VM/ESA to fully participate in open, Client/Server implementations\.
