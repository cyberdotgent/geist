### 2\.2\.1\.2 OSF/1

<a id="HDRHOSF105"></a>

OSF/1 is a UNIX\-based operating system developed by OSF\. OSF/1 has a

MACH kernel and system calls and libraries based on IBM's AIX\. OSF/1 will be available for all IBM UNIX workstations\. OSF/1 has Department of Defense \(DoD\) B1 security, POSIX 1003\.1, XPG/3, and ANSI\-C compliance\.
