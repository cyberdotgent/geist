# PREFACE Preface

The terms "Client/Server" and "Open Systems" are often found in current discussions or articles about computing\. This document answers the question of where VM/ESA fits into this new world of computing\. VM/ESA was "open" long before the term became fashionable\. As the programmable workstation liberated the professional from I/S, the need to standardize programming interfaces arose\. To get all the varied machines working together, organizations were formed to help establish new standards of computing\. It is VM/ESA's new role to comply to these new Open Systems models\. VM/ESA is committed to play a key role in this multivendor market by adapting new standards that will allow it to exploit Client/Server technology\.
