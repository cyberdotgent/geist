## 2\.2\.4 American National Standards Institute

<a id="HDRHANS100"></a>

The American National Standards Institute \(ANSI\) is a voluntary national standards coordinating and approving body\. It approves standards and accredits groups that develop standards\. It also accredits standard certification programs\. Adherence to the published standards by participating companies is voluntary\.

ANSI is a leader in coordination of OSI, graphics, and language standards\. However, it is not limited to standards for information technology\. Most notable standards are ASCII, C, COBOL, and FORTRAN\. ANSI also represents U\.S\. interests in international standards organizations\.
