### 2\.2\.1\.3 Distributed Computing Environment

<a id="HDRHOSF104"></a>

OSF's Distributed Computing Environment \(DCE\) is a comprehensive, integrated set of services that supports the development, use, and maintenance of distributed applications\. The availability of a uniform set of services anywhere in the network enables applications to effectively harness the power that tends to lie unused in many networks\. The DCE architecture draws on the submitted architectures of a multitude of organizations, including Digital Equipment Corporation, SUN Microsystems, Hewlett\-Packard, and MIT project Athena\. Though the DCE architecture has its roots in the UNIX environment, and many vendors implementing DCE are doing so on UNIX\-based operating systems, such as Digital's ULTRIX, it is by no means limited to UNIX\. Proprietary non\-UNIX\-based systems, such as IBM's MVS/ESA and VM/ESA operating systems, are being adapted for the DCE technology\.

For more information on the DCE architecture, see [Chapter 6, "Distributed](2-4.md) [Computing Environment" in topic 2\.4](2-4.md)\.
