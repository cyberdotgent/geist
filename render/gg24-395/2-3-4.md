## 2\.3\.4 Summary

<a id="HDRHDSS140"></a>

The Open Blueprint model represents a major change for IBM\. It provides the base for which multivendor heterogeneous computing is possible\. IBM has included established and emerging standards in this model to ensure interoperability with other vendors, not just with other IBM software\. The Open Blueprint provides a framework which enables IBM to respond to customer requirements with *products* and *solutions* that:

- Run on many industry hardware and software platforms, and
- Connect and work transparently with products and solutions from other industry providers\.

A major goal of the Open Blueprint is to provide a single\-system view of the network, hiding the complexities of the physical network from the user\. Its success will be measured by how well it assists customers in achieving their goals of reducing cost, improving the quality of their processes and becoming more profitable and competitive\.
