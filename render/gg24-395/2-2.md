# 2\.2 Chapter 4\. Standards and Standards Organizations

<a id="HDRHOOW100"></a>

As the world of information technology is becoming more and more complex, standards are becoming a key issue\. The "voluntary" standards organizations are the foundation of all standards activities\. They are voluntary because both participation and implementation of the resultant standards is voluntary\. This chapter lists some of the most important standards organizations and their purpose\. Other standards and standards organizations are listed in [Appendix A, "Additional Standards and](appendix1-1.md) [Standards Organizations" in topic APPENDIX1\.1](appendix1-1.md)\.
