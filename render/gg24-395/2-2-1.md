## 2\.2\.1 OSF

<a id="HDRHOSF100"></a>

The Open Software Foundation \(OSF\), founded in 1988, is a not\-for\-profit

research and development organization with more than 300 international world\-wide members, with IBM being a founding member\. The OSF's mission is to develop specifications, leadership, and software, and make available a general\-purpose vendor\-neutral open software environment to the industry under fair and equitable licensing terms\. OSF utilizes a Request For Technology \(RFT\) process to address key open computing requirements\. Some of the key technologies available to vendors from OSF are MOTIF \(Graphical User Interface\), OSF/1, and the Distributed Computing Environment \(DCE\)\. Two technologies currently under development at OSF are Distributed Management Environment \(DME\) and the Architecture\-Neutral Distribution Format \(ANDF\)\. These are the major components of the OSF Application Environment Specification \(AES\)\.
