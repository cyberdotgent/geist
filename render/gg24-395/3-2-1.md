## 3\.2\.1 OpenEdition VM \- POSIX Support

<a id="HDRHPSX100"></a>

<a id="TBLTBLUNIQ5"></a>

![PICTURE 25](25.png)

```
 |                  The introduction of POSIX support in OpenEdition VM provides
 |                 customers and vendors an internationally supported API through
 |                 which they can easily port their POSIX compliant applications.
 |                 This section will discuss in more detail what POSIX is, the
 |                 advantages of POSIX support, and how OpenEdition VM plans to
 |                 implement POSIX in VM/ESA Version 2.

 |                 VM has a long history of guest support and software emulation.
 |                 Whether it's a second level VSE system, OS simulation under CMS,
 |                 or the new UNIX style functions provided by the POSIX support,
 |                 VM is always ready to play a key role as an application platform
```

As stated in ["POSIX" in topic 2\.2\.5\.1](2-2-5-1.md), POSIX defines a series of standard

computing services that were developed by the Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineers \(IEEE\)\. These standards have since been embraced by nearly all major computer companies, many software vendors, as well as the International Standards Organization \(ISO\), which has adopted some of the POSIX standards as official standards\.

POSIX support is part of the local operating system services section of the Open Blueprint\. We have also shaded in the applications areas on the stamp sized icon above\. By taking advantage of this support, applications can be written on several different software platforms and be moved with relative ease to any other platform that supports these interfaces\.
