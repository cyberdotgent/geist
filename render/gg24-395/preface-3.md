## PREFACE\.3 How This Document Is Organized

The document contains four parts, subdivided into chapters and appendixes, as follows:

[Part 1, "Introduction"](<#HDRHPRT100>)

This part is an introduction to Client/Server computing\. It includes the following chapters:

- [Chapter 1, "A Client/Server Overview"](1-1.md)

This chapter discusses several definitions of Client/Server and its customer expectations\. Later, details on implementation, the challenges of Client/Server, and computer\-system downsizing are discussed\.

- [Chapter 2, "VM and SAA Retrospective"](1-2.md)

This chapter introduces VM/ESA and positions it with respect to early Open System requirements\.

[Part 2, "Overview of the Open World"](<#HDRHPRT200>)

This part broadens the field of Client/Server introduced in Part 1 to include details of Open Systems and the standards and organizations that create them\. As this part concludes, IBM's commitment to Open Systems is discussed\. It includes the following chapters:

- [Chapter 3, "A Closer Look at the Open World"](2-1.md)

This chapter contains a definition of what an Open Enterprise is and how this relates to Client/Server\.

- [Chapter 4, "Standards and Standards Organizations"](2-2.md)

There are many standards and organizations that are affecting the Open Systems model\. This chapter discusses the most accepted of the organizations and their standards\.

- [Chapter 5, "The Open Blueprint"](2-3.md)

This chapter discusses the Open Blueprint model and its components\.

- [Chapter 6, "Distributed Computing Environment"](2-4.md)

This chapter describes the Distributed Computing Environment \(DCE\) model and the blocks that comprise it\.

- [Chapter 7, "Yes, We're Open"](2-5.md)

This chapter shows IBM's commitment to the Open Systems standards and discusses IBM current participation and compliance\.

[Part 3, "VM and Client/Server"](<#HDRHPRT400>)

This part fits VM/ESA and its products and services into the Open Systems models\. It concludes with VM/ESA's future strategy\. It includes the following chapters:

- [Chapter 8, "VM in Today's World"](3-1.md)

This chapter maps the Client/Server requirements onto the Open Blueprint model\.

- [Chapter 9, "Opening up VM with OpenEdition"](3-2.md)

This chapter gives an overview of the new functions added in VM/ESA Version 2 that open VM to new possibilities in distributed computing\. This support includes POSIX, DCE, and the CMS GUI\.

- [Chapter 10, "VM Client/Server Products and Components"](3-3.md)

This chapter gives an overview of the products and components within VM/ESA that enable Client/Server computing\.

- [Chapter 11, "What's Next"](3-4.md)

This chapter discusses what additional compliance is required and includes direction for VM/ESA\.

[Part 4, "Appendixes"](<#HDRHPRT500>)

This part provides the appendixes that contain additional information\. It includes the following:

- [Appendix A, "Additional Standards and Standards Organizations"](appendix1-1.md)

This appendix details a list of important bodies involved in the process of defining and developing standards in the area of information technology\.

- [Appendix B, "VM Languages, Protocols, and Interoperability"](appendix1-2.md)

This appendix summarizes the VM protocols, languages, and details interoperability scenarios\.
