### 3\.3\.12\.2 DataHub/VM Software Requirements

<a id="HDRHDHB102"></a>

DataHub/VM is a feature of DB2/VM\. Further requirements for DataHub/VM are: ACF/VTAM for VM/ESA, C/370 Runtime Library, and DataHub/2 \(5667\-134\) as the programmable workstation control point\.
