### 3\.3\.6\.2 SystemView Products

<a id="HDRHSVW103"></a>

SystemView applications are available for small local area networks, large mainframes at a single site, or multiple sites connected through a network\. Products that provide system management tasks are listed in this section\. Those that are SystemView conforming are listed first\. Products with references to other sections in this book were felt to have a relation to Client/Server\. Other products have their general information manuals mentioned\.

The following are products that are considered to be SystemView conforming or have announced plans for conformance:

**ADSTAR Distributed Storage Manager \(ADSM\)**

Product number: 5648\-020 Refer to Section ["ADSTAR Distributed Storage Manager" in topic 3\.3\.11](3-3-11.md)\.

**ESCON Manager \(ESCM\)**

Product number: 5688\-008

**Host Management Facilities/VM \(HMF/VM\)**

Product number: 5684\-157 Refer to Section ["Host Management Facilities/VM" in topic 3\.3\.8](3-3-8.md)\.

**NetView**

Product number: 5756\-051 Refer to Section ["NetView" in topic 3\.3\.7](3-3-7.md)\.

**NetView Performance Monitor \(NPM\)**

Product number: 5684\-001 Refer to Section ["Associated Products" in topic 3\.3\.7\.2](3-3-7-2.md)\.

**NetView Distribution Manager \(NetView DM\)**

Product number: 5684\-017

**ScreenView**

ScreenView is a feature of VM/ESA\. Refer to Section ["ScreenView" in topic 3\.3\.18](3-3-18.md)\.

The following is a partial list of products that provide System Management functions defined by the SystemView disciplines:

**Automated Network Operations/VM \(ANO/VM\)**

Product number: 5654\-007

**DataBase 2/VM \(DB2/VM, formerly SQL/DS\)**

Product number: 5688\-004 Refer to Section ["SQL and DRDA" in topic 3\.3\.13](3-3-13.md)\.

**DataHub Support/VM**

DataHub Support/VM is a feature of DB2/VM \(5688\-103\)\. Refer to ["DataHub/VM" in topic 3\.3\.12](3-3-12.md)\.

**Data Facility System Managed Storage/VM \(DFSMS/VM\)**

DFSMS/VM is a feature of VM/ESA\. Refer to *DFSMS/VM Overview,* GC26\-4703\.

**VM/Directory Maintenance \(DIRMAINT/VM\)**

Product number: 5748\-XE4 Refer to *VM/Dirmaint Support For VM/ESA,* SC23\-0567\.

**LAN File Services/ESA \(LFS/ESA\)**

Product number: 5684\-120 Refer to Section ["LAN File Services/ESA" in topic 3\.3\.10](3-3-10.md)\.

**LAN Resource Extension and Services/VM \(LANRES/VM\)**

Product number: 5684\-142 Refer to Section ["LANRES" in topic 3\.3\.9](3-3-9.md)\.

**NetView Access Services/VM \(NetView/AS\)**

Product number: 5684\-028 Refer to Section ["Associated Products" in topic 3\.3\.7\.2](3-3-7-2.md)\.

**NetView File Transfer Program \(NetView FTP\)**

Product number: 5684\-048 Refer to Section ["Associated Products" in topic 3\.3\.7\.2](3-3-7-2.md)\.

**Resource Access Control Facility/VM \(RACF/VM\)**

Product number: 5740\-XXH Refer to *RACF Program Directory For VM Install, V1\.9\.2,* GC28\-1034\.

**Real Time Monitor/ESA \(RTM/ESA\)**

Product number: 5798\-DWD Refer to *Realtime Monitor VM/ESA Program Description/Operations,* SH26\-7000\.

**VM Distributed Systems Node Executive \(VM/DSNX\)**

Product number: 5684\-009 Refer to *VM DSNX Guide and Reference,* SC24\-5381\.

**VM Performance Reporting Facility \(VM/PRF\)**

Product number: 5684\-073 Refer to *Primer For Installing, Running, Tailoring, and Using VMPRF,* SC24\-5637\.

**VM Serviceability Enhancements Staged \(VMSES/E\)**

VMSES/E is part of VM/ESA\. Refer to *VM ESA R2 VMSES/E Introduction and Reference,* SC24\-5444\.
