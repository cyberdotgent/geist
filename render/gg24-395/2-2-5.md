## 2\.2\.5 Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers

<a id="HDRHEEE100"></a>

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers \(IEEE\) is a professional society of predominantly U\.S\. members that was formed in 1963 by the merger of the former American Institute for Electrical Engineers and the Institute of Radio Engineers\. It is accredited by ANSI to develop standards\. In fact, it was one of the founding bodies of ANSI\. Each standardization project in the IEEE is initiated by a Project Authorization Request \(PAR\), which is a request for work to be performed\. When the work is approved, the PAR becomes the authorization for that work\. The IEEE has developed or is working on several well\-known standards, including ANSI/IEEE \(POSIX 1003\.1\), IEEE 802\.3 CSMA/CD, IEEE 802\.4 Token Bus, and IEEE 802\.5 Token Ring\.
