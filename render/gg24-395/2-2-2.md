## 2\.2\.2 X/Open

<a id="HDRHXOP100"></a>

X/Open is a worldwide, independent, Open Systems organization dedicated

to developing an open, multivendor Common Application Environment \(CAE\) based on de facto and international standards\. Specification of the CAE is achieved through close cooperation between users, vendors, and standards organizations\. The *X/Open Portability Guide* is a series of books that specify the X/Open Common Application Environment\. The X/Open specification is based on IEEE/ANSI 1003\.1 \(POSIX\.1\), the System V Interface Definition \(SVID\) and other international and industry standards\. IBM is a member\.
