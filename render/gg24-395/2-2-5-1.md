### 2\.2\.5\.1 POSIX

<a id="HDRHEEE101"></a>

POSIX stands for Portable Operating System Interface for Computing

Environments\. The "X" is used because POSIX came originally out of the UNIX world\. It is a series of standards being developed by the IEEE to define standardized interfaces to common computer services\.

**Note:** Originally POSIX started as a reaction against incompatible UNIX implementations\. The broad acceptance of POSIX, however, made IEEE want to decouple POSIX from UNIX\. So IEEE so formed a new committee called *Portable Application Standards Committee* \(PASC\)\. The standards POSIX 1003\.x have been renamed PASC\.x\. In this book, will still refer to them as POSIX\. Also note that the POSIX standards are often referred to as POSIX\.x, for example, POSIX\.1 or POSIX\.2\.
