## 2\.4\.8 Management

<a id="HDRHDCE109"></a>

The DCE model shows a block called management\. This block refers to basic administration of all the other elements\. It does not, however, have the full scope of the OSF Distributed Management Environment \(DME\) model, which although frequently talked about when discussing the DCE model, has not yet been accepted by OSF\.

IBM's Open Blueprint includes Systems Management which goes much further than the basic DCE model\. Refer to Section ["Systems Management Services"](2-3-3-5.md) [in topic 2\.3\.3\.5](2-3-3-5.md) for more information\.
