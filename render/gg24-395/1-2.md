# 1\.2 Chapter 2\. VM and SAA Retrospective

<a id="HDRHPAT101"></a>

VM has been a Client/Server development platform for many years\. VM/ESA, from its beginning, has been a platform used by applications where information resources \(servers\), both local and remote, were used by its users \(clients\) in their day\-to\-day activities\. In the following chapter we describe some examples that illustrate the history of VM in the Client/Server and open arenas\.

Part one of this book will conclude by looking at SAA\. SAA can indeed be seen as a first step toward openness and interoperability, but it is limited to IBM's classical environments\.
