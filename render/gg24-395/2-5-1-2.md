### 2\.5\.1\.2 Superior Technology and Innovation

Inclusion of new and innovative technology is a key element when meeting the requirements of the open enterprise\. IBM is committed to being an active participant in the development and introduction of technology across the industry\. This will be pursued through independent research and through alliances with other vendors, customers, and industry organizations that share this goal\.
