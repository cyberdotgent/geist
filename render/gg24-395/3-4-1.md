## 3\.4\.1 VM Open Systems Strategy

<a id="HDRHFUT101"></a>

With the VM/ESA's Open Systems strategy, VM plans to continue it's commitment to the heterogeneous, multivendor environment\. VM/ESA systems will "interoperate" in environments that include hardware and software from both IBM and non\-IBM vendors\. This environment includes mainframes, intelligent workstations, terminals, and peripheral devices\. VM will continue to evaluate its interoperability characteristics as new standards become available\.

IBM is developing business partnerships with vendors that will allow VM/ESA to extend its Open Systems strategy by offering complete business solutions\. These solutions will include key attributes such as support for multivendor applications, exploitation of Client/Server technology, and compliance with industry standards\. IBM will continue to use Your Connection to VM \(YC2VM\) as a mechanism to share tools and product materials with vendors\.

VM/ESA will expand its role in Open Systems environments by enabling VM applications to participate in the heterogeneous, multivendor, distributed computing environments of the '90s\.
