## 3\.4\.3 POSIX Compliance

<a id="HDRHFUT103"></a>

Again, OpenEdition VM has added significant function to support many of the POSIX standards\. The next step for VM/ESA V2 is to become certified for POSIX\.1 by the National Institute of Standards and Technology \(NIST\)\. As well, the addition of fork\(\) will be necessary to complete the POSIX\.1 compliance\.

**Note:** For more information about POSIX, refer to Section ["OpenEdition VM](3-2-1.md) [POSIX Support" in topic 3\.2\.1](3-2-1.md)\.

As additional POSIX standards and other worldwide open standards become formally approved, IBM will evaluate the applicability of those standards on VM/ESA and will implement them as appropriate\.
