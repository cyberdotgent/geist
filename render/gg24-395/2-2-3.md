## 2\.2\.3 International Organization for Standardization

<a id="HDRISO100"></a>

The International Organization for Standardization \(ISO\), founded in

1947, is the international body responsible for adopting standards in a wide variety of fields\. ISO's membership is limited to country standards organizations, and provides a common ground for European, North American, and Asian organizations\. ISO has joined with the International Electrotechnical Commission \(IEC\) to form ISO/IEC Joint Technical Committee 1 for development of information technology standards\.
