## 2\.4\.3 Time Services

<a id="HDRHDCE104"></a>

Within a distributed computing environment, parts of the application will be executing on distinctly separate systems\. The objective of the Time Services is to ensure that there is a uniform view of time across all the distributed systems\. This is important for many applications such as a distributed file system\.

The time service consists of a client part, known as the Time Clerk, and a server part, called a Time Server\. Because no two clocks ever keep exactly the same time, the clocks across distributed systems tend to drift apart\. The Time Servers synchronize the time between them\. However, since the synchronization process naturally includes a level of network time, the answer to the question "what's the time?" is never strictly accurate\. For this reason, distributed time is expressed as a range rather than an exact time\. The time is synchronized to Universal Time, Coordinated \(UTC\)\.
