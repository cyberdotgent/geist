```
                   As mentioned in Sections "Integrated Infrastructure" in
                   topic 2.5.1.3 and "Management" in topic 2.4.8, the SystemView
                   framework is IBM's systems and network management strategy for
                   planning, coordinating, and operating open, heterogeneous,
```
