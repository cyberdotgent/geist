### 2\.5\.1\.1 Commitment to Standards

IBM maintains an unswerving commitment to the creation, acceptance, and adherence to information technology standards; those that are already accepted, common in the industry \(de facto\), or emerging\. In addition, IBM will actively offer IBM technology to the standards process, and work with customers, research institutes, other vendors, and industry consortia to promote the definitions and availability of open technology standards\.
