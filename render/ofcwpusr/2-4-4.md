### 2.4.4 Typing Margin Text with Paragraphs

To type text in the margin next to a paragraph: 1. Type the margin text for the first line of the paragraph. 2. Press the Tab key. This takes you to the first tab stop. 3. Type the first line of the paragraph, and press the Field Exit key. 4. Repeat the above steps for each line with text in the margin. 5. Press the Alt key plus the Tab key to insert a Required Tab control

character. 6. Complete typing the remainder of the paragraph. The remaining lines

of the paragraph are automatically indented. 7. Press the Field Exit key at the end of the paragraph.

Lines that end with a Required Carrier Return (Field Exit) will not

be right justified if right justification is selected on the Line

Spacing/Justification display.
