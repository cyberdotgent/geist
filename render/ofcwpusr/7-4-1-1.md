#### Changing the Page Layout and Paper Options for the Entire Document To change the page layout and paper options for the entire document:

1. Press F5 (Goto) to show the Goto prompt.

2. Type dp in the prompt and press the Enter key.

Page 1 of the Page Layout/Paper Options display is shown.
