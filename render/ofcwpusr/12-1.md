## 12.1 Typing Numbers to Be Processed By Summary Math

Calculation on a row of numbers starts to the left of the instruction and continues from right to left until no more numbers or the start of a line is found. Therefore, the Summary Math instruction should be typed to the right of the row of numbers. The numbers in the row must be separated by

one or more blanks or a tab.

Calculation on a column of numbers starts on the line above the instruction and continues up until no more numbers are found. Therefore, the Summary Math instruction should be under at least one digit of each

number in the column
