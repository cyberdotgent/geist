### 3.11.2 Inserting Syllable Hyphens

You can either insert syllable hyphens manually as you type, or you can have the system insert them automatically. Syllable hyphens are inserted

between syllables of a word based on the hyphenation points already

defined in the dictionaries; for example, syl-la-ble
