To move the cursor to the bottom of the page shown on the display, press the Alt key and the Cursor Down key. (If the cursor is already at the bottom of the page, the bottom of the following page is shown.) To move the cursor to the end of a line of text, press the Alt key and the

Cursor Right key. To move the cursor to the beginning of a line of text, press the Alt key

and the Cursor Left key.
