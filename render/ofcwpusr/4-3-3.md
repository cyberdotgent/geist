### Adding Words to a Dictionary

To add words to a dictionary:

<a id="ELISone"></a>

1. Position the cursor to the word you want to add to the dictionary.

| 2. Press F5 and type aw. Press the Enter key.

The Add Word box is shown on the Edit display., c.cp

3. Type a slash where you want the hyphens to be.

| ................................................

| : Add Word :

| : :

| : hyphenation points, press Enter. :

| : :

| : dic/tio/nary :

| : :

| :..............................................

4. Press the Enter key and receive the following message:

Word will be added to dictionary 'xxxxxxxxxx'

Dictionary 'xxxxxxxxxx' is the name of the dictionary you specified on

| the Spell Options display for the Dictionary for adding words prompt.

[5. Repeat steps 1 through 4 for each word you want to add to the](index.md)

dictionary. | 6. Press F3 (Exit) or F12 (Cancel) when you have finished adding the

words to your dictionary.

This dictionary is not actually created until you end your office session. Then you must go to the Dictionary Options display to activate the dictionary before you can use it to check the spelling in

your document.
