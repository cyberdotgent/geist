To insert Required Spaces for the above example:

1. After you type the y in July, press the Alt key plus the spacebar. 2. After the comma (,), press the Alt key plus the spacebar. To check that a Required Space is inserted between the words, move your

cursor between the y and the 4. The words Required Space should appear in

the audit window at the top of the Edit display.

Notes: 1. Required Spaces are not be expanded during line justification.

2. Required Spaces are handled like text characters. 3. Required Spaces are always struck over by the Overstrike text

instruction. 4. Required Spaces are underlined by a Word Underline control character.
