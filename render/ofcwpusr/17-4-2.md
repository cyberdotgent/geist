Column areas that do not contain text (null column areas) cannot be copied, moved, or deleted. If you try to mark these areas for copy, move, or delete (including Sort and Notepad functions), you get an error message. In the following example, the area after column 2 (a column that is shorter than the surrounding columns) is a null column area.
