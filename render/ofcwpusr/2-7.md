## 2.7 Typing Superscripts and Subscripts

Superscript characters print a half space above the normal printing line.

Subscript characters print a half space below the normal printing line.

When using superscript or subscript characters, you may want to change the line spacing to a space and a half or double spacing in order to prevent

the superscript or subscript character from printing on top of the lines

[of text above or below it. For more information, see "Changing the Line](7-3-1.md)
