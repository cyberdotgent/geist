## Moving Around in the Document Use F5 (Goto) to find a specific page or line in your document. You can use F4 (Find character) or F6 (Find) to find a specific character, word, or phrase in your document. Use F18 (Search/Replace) to find a word or

phrase and replace it with another word or phrase
