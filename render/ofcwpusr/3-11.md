## 3.11 Specifying Words to Be Hyphenated

There are two types of hyphens:

Required hyphens are used to connect two or more words to form a

compound word; for example, father-in-law. These hyphens are never

removed by the system.

Syllable hyphens are inserted between syllables of a word at the end of a line. These hyphens are added and/or removed by the system as

necessary during line adjustment and pagination. You can either insert syllable hyphens as you are typing or you can

let the system automatically hyphenate words for you.

If you are using the PC text-assist function, you must insert syllable hyphens as you are typing or after you have typed the

word. Automatic hyphenation is not supported
