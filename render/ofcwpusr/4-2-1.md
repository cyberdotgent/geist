You must select the name of the language dictionary and any other

dictionaries you want to use before you can use any of the spell functions. The most common way to check and correct your spelling

is from the Spell Options display.

To check and correct misspelled words: | 1. Press F22 (Spell functions) to show the Spell Functions display. | 2. Select option 1 (Spell options) and press the Enter key to show the

| Spell Options display.

3. Select option 3 (Check document and locate marks) for the Option to

perform prompt. If you do not want to locate misspelled words in the entire document,

specify the starting and ending pages to be searched.

4. Type a Y (Yes) for the Prompted aid box prompt. A list of possible

spellings is automatically displayed for each misspelled word found.
