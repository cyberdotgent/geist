## 1\.22 Changing Multiple Occurrences of a Meeting

You can change multiple occurrences of a meeting by doing the following:

1\. On the Daily, Weekly, or Six Month Calendar display, press F13 \(More tasks\)\. The More Calendar Tasks display appears\.

2\. For the *Selection* prompt, type a **7** \(Work with Meetings\)\. Press the Enter key\.

3\. The Work with Meetings display appears\.

4\. Move the cursor down to the meeting you want to change and type a **2** in the *Opt* column\. Press the Enter key\. The Change Meeting display appears\.

5\. On the Change Meeting display, press F10 \(Change all occurrences\)\.

The Change All Occurrences display appears\.
