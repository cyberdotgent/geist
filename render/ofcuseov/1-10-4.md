### 1.10.4 Changing a Meeting

Sometimes it is necessary to change information about a scheduled meeting. For example, you may need to change the time, place, or have additional people attend. You may also need to delete a meeting. To change the meeting, you must be the person who requested or scheduled it. To change a meeting (multiple occurring or not) without accessing the

Work with Meetings display, see ["Changing a Meeting" in topic 1.21](1-21.md). To change a meeting, do the following: 1. On the Daily, Weekly, or Six Month Calendar display, press F13 (More tasks). The More Calendar Tasks display appears.

**Note:** You can also do this function on the Daily, Weekly, or Six

Month Calendar display by typing WM (Work wi**th** Meetings) for the Function pr*ompt and* pressing the Enter key. 2. For the *Selection* prompt, type **a** 7 (Work with Meetings). Press the Enter key. 3. The Work with Meetings display appears.
