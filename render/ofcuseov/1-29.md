## 1\.29 Working with a Calendar Group

<a id="HDRWCALGRP"></a>

A **calendar group** is a list of calendars used to schedule meetings for a group of users in a single operation\.

**Note:** A distribution list can also be used to identify a calendar group\.

This topic describes how to create a calendar group, delete a calendar group, add users to a calendar group, use a Group Calendar display, and use a Composite Calendar display\. For information about how to schedule a meeting for all members of a calendar group, see ["Scheduling a Meeting" in](1-10.md) [topic 1\.10](1-10.md)\.
