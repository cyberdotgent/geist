## 1.10.2 Finding a Place

<a id="HDRFNDPLC"></a>

You can find an available meeting place by finding the available times on calendars that represent a meeting place, such as a conference room. To find an available meeting place, do the following: 1. On the Add Meeting or Change Meeting display, press F13 (Find place).
