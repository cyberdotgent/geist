## 1.7 Changing a Calendar Session

Changing a calendar session allows you to temporarily or permanently change the defaults (system-supplied values) or values entered by the administrator for your calendar user profile. The defaults are established when you enroll. The defaults, depending on the type of calendar, include the start time, time format, view type, column/days, time interval, start day, and number of days.
