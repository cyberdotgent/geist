# 1\.0 Chapter 1\. Using Calendar Functions

<a id="HDRUSECAL"></a>

This chapter describes how to use the calendar functions included with OfficeVision/400\. The calendar function allows you to keep track of items such as events, meetings, reminders, notes, jobs, and procedures for yourself or for someone who has authorized you to his calendar\. You can also schedule meetings on several calendars you are authorized to by using a calendar group or distribution list\.
