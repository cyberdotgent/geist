## 6\.4 Working with Personal Directories

A **personal directory** is a collection of entries of similar things and is defined by the user\. For example, a personal directory can be a telephone directory or an inventory report\. A personal directory that you create may or may not have anything to do with the system distribution directory, depending on what you intend to use the personal directory for\.
