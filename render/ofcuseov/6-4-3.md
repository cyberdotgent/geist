### 6.4.3 Changing User Authority to a Personal Directory

1. On the Directories and Distribution Lists display, type option **1** (Personal directories). Press the Enter key. 2. The Specify Personal Directory Criteria display appears. If your administrator has specified a default personal directory for you, your display appears slightly different from the following display. Press F13 (More tasks).
