### 5.4.5 Deleting a Document List

To delete a document list, do the following:

1. On the Work with Documents and Folders display, type option **4** (Work with document lists). Press the Enter key. 2. The Work with Owned Document Lists display appears. 3. Move the cursor next to the description that you want to delete and in the Opt *col*umn, type a 4 (D**e**lete list). Press the Enter key. 4. The Confirm Delete of Document Lists display appears. This display

shows which document list you selected to delete. Confirm Delete of Document Lists Press Enter to confirm your choices for 4=Delete. Press F12 to return to change your choices. Opt Description List Date Run 4 Department information DEPTINFO 03/15/88

Bottom F12=Cancel 5. Press the Enter key to confirm the deletion of the list, or press F12 (Cancel) to return to the Work with Owned Document Lists display, where you can make another selection. 6. Press F3 (Exit).
