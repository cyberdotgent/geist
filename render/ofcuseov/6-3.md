## 6.3 Working with Nicknames

<a id="HDRWRKNCK"></a>

A **nickname** is a short version of the *User* *ID* and *Address* or the *List* *ID* and *List* *ID* *qualifier* prompts on a display. Instead of typing all the information for a user or a distribution list, an existing nickname can be typed for the *User* *ID* or *List* *ID* prompt. Nicknames are personal, not public, and are not shared with other users.
