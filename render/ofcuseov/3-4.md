## 3.4 Replying to a Message

Sometimes it may be necessary to reply to a message. When you do, the original message stays in your mail log without your reply. To reply to a message, do the following: 1. On the Work with Mail display, move the cursor down to the message you

want to reply to. Type an 11 (**Re**ply) and press the Enter key.
