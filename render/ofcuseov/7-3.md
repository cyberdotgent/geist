## 7.3 Permitting Others to Handle Your Mail and Filed Documents

You can either give or revoke (remove) the authority of a person to handle your mail and filed documents by doing the following:

1. On the Administration display, type option **3** (Permit others to handle mail/filed documents) and press the Enter key.
