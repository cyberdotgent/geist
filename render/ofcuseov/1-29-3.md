### 1.29.3 Deleting a Calendar Group

To delete a calendar group, do the following: 1. On the Daily, Weekly, Group, Composite, or Six Month Calendar display, press F13 (More calendar tasks). The More Calendar Tasks display appears.

2. For the Selection prompt, type a 3 (Work with groups). 3. Press the Enter key. The Work with Groups display appears.
