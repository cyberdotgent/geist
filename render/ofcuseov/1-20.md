## 1.20 Changing Multiple Occurrences of a Calendar Item

<a id="HDRMLTO"></a>

You can change multiple occurrences of a calendar item on your calendar by doing the following: 1. On the Daily or Weekly Calendar display, move the cursor to a calendar item for a multiple occurrence you want to change and press F10 (Change item). If the item is an event, the Change Event display appears. If the item is something other than an event, then a similar display appears.
