## 2.5 Viewing the Outgoing Mail Status

This topic shows you how to view the status of mail items that you have sent to other people. Outgoing mail status includes such things as the date the item was sent, who received it, and what was done with it. To view the status of the outgoing mail, do the following: 1. On the Work with Mail display, press F6 (Outgoing mail status). 2. The Work with Outgoing Mail Status display appears with the
