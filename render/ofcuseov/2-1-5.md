## 2.1.5 Forwarding a Mail Item

<a id="HDRFWDMIL"></a>

By **forwarding** a mail item, you can send a copy of a mail item you have received to someone else without having to retype it. You can also add your comments about the mail item (by sending a note or memo slip) and forward them with the mail item. To forward a mail item, do the following: 1. On the Work with Mail display, move the cursor down to the mail item you want to forward. Type a 10 (**Fo**rward) and press the Enter key. 2. The Forward Mail display appears. Notice a description of the mail item you are forwarding is shown at the top of the display. Also, notice that the Subj*ect and* Refe*rence inf*ormation for this mail item is shown.
