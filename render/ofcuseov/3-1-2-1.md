## 3.1.2.1 Attaching a Memo Slip to a Message

<a id="HDRMESMEM"></a>

#### Attaching a Memo Slip to a Message

message. To attach a memo slip to a message, do the following: 1. On the Send a Message display, press F9 (Attach memo slip). Attach Memo Slip Type choices, press Enter. Action . . . . . . . . . _ 1=For your information 2=For your comments 3=For your signature 4=For your approval 5=Please handle 6=Please circulate 7=Please see me 8=Please prepare reply .. F3=Exit F5=Refresh F12=Cancel F19=Display messages 3. Type one of the choices for the *Action* prompt. 4. Press the Enter key. The Send a Message display appears. 5. Press F10 (Send) to send the message. 6. Press F3 (Exit).
