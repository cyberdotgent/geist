## 3.2 Viewing a Message

To view a message, do the following:

1. On the OfficeVision/400 menu, type option **2** (Mail) and press the Enter key. 2. The Work with Mail display appears. You can tell which items on the display are messages because they have a status of message. The message status remains the same until you delete the message.
