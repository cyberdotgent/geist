#### 6\.3\.1\.4 YAM Serial Port TNC

It is unlikely this will work straight away in this modern age, but this entry is provided for completeless:

```
     INTERFACE=4
             TYPE=YAM
             COM=COM1
             SPEED=9600
             PROTOCOL=HDLC
             MTU=256
     ENDINTERFACE
```
