#### 6.3.1.9 PPP Serial Port Interface

This configures a PPP interface that supports LCP, IPCP, and IPv4.

INTERFACE=6

TYPE=ASYNC

COM=COM1

SPEED=9600

PROTOCOL=PPP

FLOW=0

MTU=1500

ENDINTERFACE
