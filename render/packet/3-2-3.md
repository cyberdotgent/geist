##### 3.2.3 Listening and Beaconing

Inevitably, you will want to discover adjacent NET/ROM nodes, listen for AX.25 packets on the air, and send out beacons. To discover adjacent

$ ``c`at` `/proc/net/nr_nodes` $ ``c`at` `/proc/net/nr_neigh`

# ``l`isten` `-c` `-a`

If you wish to view a list of stations you have heard so far, invoke this (note: if you are tuned to the APRS frequency, you may get hundreds of

lines of output in a busy city): $ ``m`heard`

To send out a beacon announcing your presence, you can do so by transmitting AX.25 UI-frames on a fixed timer (this transmits it on the

AX.25 port named radio): $ ``b`eac`on`` `-t` 10 `radio` `"WA4XYZ` `Packet` ``Radio`` Node" While connected, you can use netstat to get information about active

$ ``n`etstat` `--ax25` $ ``n`etstat` `--netrom` $ ``n`etstat` `--rose`
