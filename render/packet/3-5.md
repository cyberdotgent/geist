### 3.5 AXIP and AXUDP Tunnels

Sometimes, it is necessary to bridge a packet radio network over the Internet (or some other network link that speaks TCP/IP natively, and does not natively support AX.25). In order to do this, one can construct an **AX/IP** tunnel -- this encapsulates the AX.25 frames into IPv4 packets. Likewise, to encapsulate TCP/IP into UDP packets, one can create an **AX/UDP** tunnel. [(23)](#FTNFTNUNIQ34)
