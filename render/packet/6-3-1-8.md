#### 6.3.1.8 SLIP Serial Port Interface

$ This provides a Serial Line IP interface without the aid of a dial-up modem; this is a wireline connection with a null-modem connection.

INTERFACE=6

TYPE=ASYNC

COM=COM1

SPEED=9600

PROTOCOL=SLIP

FLOW=0

MTU=1500

ENDINTERFACE
