#### 6.3.1.5 NET/ROM Switch Serial Interface

This permits communication with a traditional *NET/ROM* *TNC*, or a null-modem serial cable to another NET/ROM switch port:

```text
INTERFACE=4
TYPE=ASYNC
COM=COM1
SPEED=19200
PROTOCOL=NETROM
MTU=256
ENDINTERFACE
```
