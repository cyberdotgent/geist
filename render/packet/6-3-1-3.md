#### 6.3.1.3 WA8DED Host-Mode Serial Port TNC

This is a non-KISS TNC:

```text
INTERFACE=3
TYPE=ASYNC
COM=COM1
SPEED=9600
PROTOCOL=DEDHOST
FLOW=0
CHANNELS=4
MTU=256
ENDINTERFACE
```
