#### 6.3.1.6 XRouter As a TNC2 Emulation

This simulates the user interface of a standard TNC2, with the cmd: prompt

and all the associated commands:

INTERFACE=5

TYPE=ASYNC

COM=COM1

SPEED=9600

PROTOCOL=TNC2

MTU=256

ENDINTERFACE
