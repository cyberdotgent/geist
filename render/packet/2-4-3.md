* A key part of both IPv4 and IPv6 is the concept of discovering what * Ethernet MAC address/AX.25 callsign/PPP peer maps to an IP address. IPv4 ***does this with a protocol called ARP, the Address Resolution Protocol***. ***IPv6 doe***s*** this wit***h the Neigh***bor Di***scovery subset of ICMPv6.
