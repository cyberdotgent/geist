### 2.5 TexNet

When you read the AX.25 Protocol Number list above, you probably saw *TexNet on that list! This is a defunct protocol that is similar to NET/ROM, and was used for the Texas/Oklahoma/Arkansas packet network of* *th*e *sam*e name. Nearly all sites on TexNet were 9600 baud stations, in the *mid-1980s! Needless to say, though it is defunct, its position in the development of NET/ROM is important to at least note*.
