### 2.3 ROSE

*The *R*ATS* Open Systems Environment is a direct port of the X.25 PLP (Packet -- Layer Protocol) to run on top of AX.25. Like the original X.25 PLP, ROSE ***uses the standard 10-digit X.25 addressing scheme (frequently called X.121 addresses) and requires manual mapping of callsigns to network numbers***.

Unfortunately, ROSE was not very well-adopted; the Linux kernel's AX.25 stack contains the most complete implementation of ROSE, but even it is buggy and not worth running.
