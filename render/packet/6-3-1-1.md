#### 6.3.1.1 Internal Loopback

This is the equivalent of the loopback device on a UNIX TCP/IP stack: INTERFACE=1 TYPE=LOOPBACK PROTOCOL=KISS MTU=576 ENDINTERFACE The protocol used here is **KISS**, as it will need to speak *any* protocol.
