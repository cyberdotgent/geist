##### 2.2.3 NET/ROM Serial Protocol

When the original NET/ROM-capable TNC ROMs became available in the -- mid-80s, KISS mode was *th*e de *facto* standard for driving TNCs; for efficiency reasons, several hams realized they could drop the AX.25 encapsulation and *just* carry NET/ROM packets to and from the TNC. This

became known as **the** NRS protocol.
