## 7.1 The LoRa Protocol

LoRa short for Long Range, is an ISM-band based protocol that is intended to provide excellent range (compared to other modulation modes, like AX.25's Bell 103 modem) for the amount of transmit power used (which, as one might imagine being on an ISM band, is in the milliwatts). [(62)](#FTNFTNUNIQ76)
