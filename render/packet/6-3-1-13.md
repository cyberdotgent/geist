#### 6.3.1.13 AXTCP Server Interface

To accept AX.25-over-TCP connections from remote systems:

INTERFACE=4

TYPE=AXTCP

MTU=1300

INTNUM=9393

ENDINTERFACE

INTNUM is the port to listen on.
