## BIBLIOGRAPHY\.8 OS/2 EE V1\.3 Information

Publications are included in the IBM OS/2 Version 1\.3 Programming Tools and Information product package\. For ordering assistance, contact your IBM authorized dealer or your IBM marketing representative\.

Of the information included with the OS/2 program diskettes, the following may be particularly helpful when installing and using the NetView Graphic Monitor Facility\.

*OS/2 EE V1\.3 Getting Started*

*OS/2 EE V1\.3 User's Guide, Volume 1: Base Operating System*

In addition, the following separately orderable publications contain information relevant to users of the NetView Graphic Monitor Facility\.

*OS/2 Information and Planning Guide* \(G360\-2650\)

*OS/2 EE V1\.3 Programming Services and Advanced Problem Determination for Communications* \(01F0299\)

*OS/2 EE V1\.3 Problem Determination Guide for the Service Coordinator* \(01F0301 \(US\); 87F1090\)

*OS/2 EE V1\.3 System Administrator's Guide for Communications* \(01F0302\)
