## 2.1 Backing Up the Option/Driver Diskette

<a id="HDRBODD"></a>

The Option/Driver Diskette came with your adapter.

- **1.** With the original Option/Driver Diskette in the drive and your computer on, use the DISKCOPY command to make a backup copy of the diskette (the target diskette is the backup diskette). Store the original diskette in a safe place.
- **2.** Remove the backup copy of the Option/Driver Diskette from the drive (and the original if it is in a drive).
- 3. Go to ["Updating the Backup Copy of Your Reference Diskette" in](2-2.md) [topic 2.2](2-2.md).
