# 3\.0 Chapter 3\. Problem Solving

<a id="HDRPROBSOV"></a>

This chapter contains procedures that help you troubleshoot problems with a Dual EtherStreamer MC 32 Adapter\.

It is important to note that either one of the two ports on the Dual EtherStreamer MC 32 Adapter could be operating normally while the other port is not\. Likewise, both ports could require troubleshooting for different reasons\.
