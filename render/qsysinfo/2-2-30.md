### 2.2.30 GG22-9125, Communications Systems Bulletin TCP/IP

This publication provides a basic understanding of Transmission Control ___ Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) and IBM's products that implement it. In addition, this document is intended to provide information relating TCP/IP to other IBM products, including Systems Network Architecture (SNA)and OSI. This manual also includes an overview of applications, connectivity, TCP/IP networking, and heterogeneous networks. *175* *pp.*
