### 2\.1\.55 SC41\-0006, PC Support/400: DOS Installation and Administration Guide

*Publication Description*: *PC Support/400 DOS Installation and Administration Guide* provides information for planning and installing PC Support/400, and configuring and diagnosing problems for individual PC Support/400 users\. This manual is intended for users with personal computers using the DOS operating system\. *550 pp\.*

*Related Publications*: *PC Support/400: DOS and OS/2 Technical Reference*, SC41\-8091

*Intended Audience*: System administrator, supervisor

*Distribution Information*: Included with program 5738\-PC1, in binder 43; $50\.00 for additional copies\.
