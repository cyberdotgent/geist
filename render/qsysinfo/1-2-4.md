### 1\.2\.4 Online Education

Online education is supplied in the form of tutorials to help you learn about your AS/400 system online\. To use the tutorials:

- Select option 10 \(User support and education\) on the AS/400 Main Menu, press F13 \(User support\) on any system menu, or enter *Go Support* on any command line\.
- On the User Support menu, select option 9 \(Online education\)\.
- Press the Enter key\.

There are three types of online education available on the AS/400 system:

- Tutorial System Support
- Additional IBM online education
- Online education

See the *System Operator's Guide*, SC41\-8082, for more information on using or administering online education\.
