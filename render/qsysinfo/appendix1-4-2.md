## APPENDIX1\.4\.2 Publications Shipped with Licensed Programs

The publications associated with the operating system and some other licensed programs have been grouped into features according to tasks or system functions so you can choose only those manuals applicable to your needs\.

When you order the licensed program\(s\), you also specify the codes for the groups of manuals you want\. Feature codes beginning with a 7 are available at no charge \(for the first set\); feature codes beginning with an 8 [\(1\)](<#FTNFEAT>) are chargeable\. The following tables indicate the cost for each feature\.

For each licensed program, the first manual listed is the licensed program specification for that program\. These specifications provide a description, as well as warranty information and operating requirements, for the program\.

<a id="FTNFEAT"></a>

\(1\) Feature codes and costs shown are applicable to US; availability and costs may be different in other countries\.
