### 2\.2\.46 GG24\-3249, System/36 System Migration to AS/400

This manual provides information for migrating configurations, security, business graphics utility \(BGU\), advanced printer function \(APF\), and intelligent printer data stream \(IDPS\)\. It also discusses operational differences from the System/36 to the AS/400 system\. *125 pp\.*
