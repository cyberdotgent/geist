### 2\.2\.53 GG24\-3288, System/3X and AS/400 APPN Nodes Using the SNA/LEN Subarea Network

This manual provides programmers and systems engineers in the intermediate systems and VTAM/NCP area with information on the incorporation of a System/370 systems network architecture \(SNA\) subarea into a network of advanced peer\-to\-peer network \(APPN\) nodes\. *175 pp\.*
