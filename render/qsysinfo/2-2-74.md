### 2\.2\.74 GG24\-3519, Coexistence of AS/400 PC Support/400 with PC Local Area Network Programs

This manual provides an overview of the co\-existence of the local area network \(LAN\) products running under DOS or the OS/2 program with AS/400 PC Support/400 in a token\-ring network environment\. It contains tips and technical topics regarding the implementation of LAN licensed programs; however, this document is not intended to cover these products in detail\. This edition applies to Release 3\.0 of AS/400 PC Support/400 \(Program Number 5728\-PC1\) and to all subsequent releases until otherwise indicated in new editions or technical bulletins\. *275 pp\.*
