### 2\.2\.6 GA21\-9607, Systems Application Architecture SystemView and the AS/400 System

This manual begins with an overview of the SystemView strategy for systems management\. It describes the functions available for systems management with the AS/400 system and presents some business solutions to show how the AS/400 system can act as a managing or managed system in a network environment\. The growth of the AS/400 SystemView offerings is discussed to provide an idea of the future direction of the AS/400 system in the area of systems management\. *50 pp\.*
