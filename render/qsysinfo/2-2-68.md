### 2.2.68 GG24-3452, AS/400 Printing

This manual includes information on the AS/400 printing functions available on a range of supported printers in different environments. It is intended for anyone interested in, or involved in, the planning and implementation of printing applications using the AS/400 system. A basic

knowledge of computing is assumed. *250* *pp.*
