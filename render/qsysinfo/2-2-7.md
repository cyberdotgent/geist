### 2.2.7 GA21-9990, Application System/400:  a Business Perspective

This publication describes the various support programs provided by the Rochester, Minnesota, Marketing and Support Center for the AS/400 system and 5363 products: executive briefings, system design reviews, special bid support, critical situation support, and skills transfer activities. It is intended for marketing representatives and systems engineers. *300* *pp.*
