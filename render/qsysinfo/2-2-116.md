### 2.2.116 SV32-0608, System/38 Environment

This videotape provides the programmer with information on the capabilities and implementation of the AS/400 System/38 environment. This includes information about the functions of the environment, commands and command processing, and differences between System/38 on the AS/400 system and the System/38. *1/2* *inch,* *VHS,* *NTSC,* *46* *minutes.*
