### 2\.2\.72 GG24\-3496, AS/400 \- OS/2 Ethernet Connectivity

This publication describes the presently available connections between OS/2 Extended Edition 1\.2 and the AS/400 system via the 8209 LAN Bridge\. The 8209 LAN Bridge provides, among other functions, a bridge between the PS/2 systems attached to an Ethernet LAN and an AS/400 system attached to a token\-ring LAN\. This publication is intended for persons who need to configure and use 5250 workstation feature, 3270 device emulation, and AS/400 PC Support/400 for connectivity, via the 8209 LAN Bridge Basic knowledge of OS/2 Extended Edition 1\.2, AS/400 PC Support/400, and the AS/400 system is assumed\. *225 pp\.*
