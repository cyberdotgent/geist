### 2\.1\.155\.1 SX21\-9065, AS/400 Standard 3\-Ring Binder \(2 Inches with Pockets\)

This binder is covered in white vinyl, with a full\-length clear pocket on the spine and vinyl pockets on the inside covers\. The outside cover has **Application System/400** printed at the top and a raised IBM logo across the front\. *11" x 11 1/2"\. Capacity: 480 sheets \(960 pages\)*

*Distribution Information*: Not furnished with the library; cost is $17\.00\.
