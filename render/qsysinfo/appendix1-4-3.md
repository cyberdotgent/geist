## APPENDIX1\.4\.3 Ordering Additional Publications

<a id="HDRORDER"></a>

You can order individual manuals and binders by order number, or you can order additional groups of manuals \(with or without binders\) by SBOF numbers\. The contents of the SBOF groups generally correspond to the contents of the licensed program and feature groups\. Costs of individual manuals and binders are shown in [Part 2 in topic 2\.0](<#HDRPD>)\. Costs of SBOF groups equal the sum of the parts\.

**Note:** Spine label inserts are *not* included for the binders in an SBOF group as they are when you order the corresponding feature\. However, a binder insert package \(SX41\-9073\) that contains duplicate labels for *all* the binders associated with *all* the licensed programs is also available to order\.
