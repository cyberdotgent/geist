# 2\.2 Other AS/400 Information from IBM

<a id="HDRRELI"></a>

The publications in this section, also published by IBM, are about the AS/400 system but are not part of the AS/400 library that is distributed with the licensed programs and hardware products\. The information here is correct to the best of our knowledge at press time, but keep in mind that the publications listed here may have changed or been withdrawn\. They may also not relate directly to Version 2 Release 1\.

All of these publications must be ordered separately through your local IBM branch office\. See your marketing representative for ordering information\.

This section includes the title, order number, and a brief description of each publication\. The publication descriptions are presented in order number sequence\.
