### 2\.1\.126 SC41\-9993, Central Site Distribution Guide

*Publication Description*: *Central Site Distribution Guide* provides information on how to distribute licensed programs, program temporary fixes \(PTFs\), and application programs to other systems under IBM licensing agreements\. This manual includes planning information and instructions on how to create and distribute tapes to other systems\. *75 pp\.*

*Related Publications*: *Licensed Programs and New Release Installation Guide*, SC41\-9878

*Intended Audience*: System programmer

*Distribution Information*: Included with program 5738\-SS1, feature 8010, in binder 23; $7\.50 for additional copies\.
