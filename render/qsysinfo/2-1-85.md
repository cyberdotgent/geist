### 2\.1\.85 SC41\-8211, New User's Guide

*Publication Description*: The *New User's Guide* provides beginner information about how to sign on and off; send and receive messages; respond to keyboard error messages; use function keys; use display, command, and help information; and control and manage jobs\. *150 pp\.*

*Related Publications*: \(Prerequisite for all other AS/400 information and interfaces\)

*Intended Audience*: All AS/400 users

*Distribution Information*: Included with program 5738\-SS1, in binder 1; $12\.50 for additional copies\.
