### 2.2.8 GA21-9992, AS/400, System/36, System/38 Key to Service

This manual explains software service for the System/36, System/38, or AS/400 systems as it is given by the National Service Division (NSD). This manual documents electronic customer support service functions, online PTF and information requests and receipts, Level 1/Level 2 support structure, helps for preparing to report a problem, description of suggested maintenance strategy, and description of preventive service

planning files. *50* *pp.*
