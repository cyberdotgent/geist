### 2\.2\.121 SV32\-0615, AS/400: A Programmer's View

This videotape explains the concepts that are basic to writing and maintaining programs on the AS/400 system\. Two programmers introduce four key concepts:

- System architecture
- Programming tools
- System management
- Connectivity

Animated graphics help illustrate the concepts\. The videotape is a preview for the AS/400 concepts manual\. It is designed primarily for new AS/400 programmers who are not familiar with the System/38\. This videotape may not be viewed using videotape players other than those for 1/2\-inch VHS PAL format videotape\. Other formats of this videotape are available with order numbers: SV32\-0609, 1/2\-inch VHS NTSC US and SV32\-0616, 1/2\-inch VHS SECAM\. *1/2 inch, VHS, PAL format, 60 minutes\.*
