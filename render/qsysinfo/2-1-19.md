### 2\.1\.19 SC09\-1156, Languages: PL/I User's Guide and Reference

*Publication Description*: *PL/I User's Guide and Reference* provides information about using AS/400 PL/I in the System/38 environment\. Differences between the System/38 environment and the AS/400 environment are identified as well as the enhancements available in the AS/400 environment\. *525 pp\.*

*Related Publications*: *Languages: PL/I Reference Summary*, SX09\-1051

*Intended Audience*: Application programmer

*Distribution Information*: Included with program 5738\-PL1, in binder 68; $90\.00 for additional copies\.
