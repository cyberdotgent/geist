# APPENDIX1\.4 Appendix D\. AS/400 Library Distribution and Ordering Information

<a id="HDRAPPD"></a>

When you order an AS/400 hardware product or licensed program, you are automatically shipped one copy of a basic set of associated manuals\. \(In the United States, you will also receive labeled, three\-ring binders to contain the manuals\.\) For some licensed programs, additional groups of manuals for specific topics are also available\. To receive them, you need to specify the appropriate feature codes when you order the program\.

The tables in this appendix show which manuals and binders are available with each hardware product and licensed program\. You can use the first column of the tables to check off the manuals you order or receive\.

In addition to the manuals shipped with your order, the following manuals are available from your marketing representative to assist you in planning for your system:

<a id="TBLTBLUNIQ3"></a>

```
            Order No       Title
   ___      GC41-9678      Publications Guide
   ___      GC41-9766      System Introduction
   ___      GC41-9802      System Concepts
   ___      GA41-0007      New Products Planning Information for Version 2
                           Release 1
   ___      GA41-0001      Physical Planning Guide
   ___      GA41-9571      Physical Planning Guide and Reference
   ___      GC41-9861      Network Planning Guide
   ___      GC41-9877      National Language Support Planning Guide
   ___      GC41-9623      Migrating from System/36 Planning Guide
   ___      GC41-9624      Migrating from System/38 Planning Guide
```

You can also order individual manuals or groups of manuals; see ["Ordering](appendix1-4-3.md) [Additional Publications" in topic APPENDIX1\.4\.3](appendix1-4-3.md) for information\.

If you have a question or did not receive a manual you ordered, please contact your local branch office or your marketing representative\.
