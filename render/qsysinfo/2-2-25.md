### 2\.2\.25 GC21\-9847, AS/400 Guide for Developing Usable and Consistent Applications

This guide provides novice and experienced developers with information about the entire application development cycle so that they can produce more usable and consistent applications\. The cycle begins with understanding the needs of the end user and proceeds through to the developed application\. *164 pp\.*
