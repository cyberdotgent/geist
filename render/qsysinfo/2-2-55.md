### 2\.2\.55 GG24\-3304, Converting System/36 Environment Applications to Native AS/400

This manual provides information for converting System/36 applications to the AS/400 system\. This manual also includes information on tools and approaches used in the conversion process\. It is intended for System/36 and AS/400 planners and users\. *250 pp\.*
