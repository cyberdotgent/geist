### 2.2.96 SC21-9846, AS/400 Intermediate Process for Service and Support

This publication is written for intermediates, including IBM's business partners and organizations that have more than one System/3x and that provide a central help desk. It describes how business partners can service and provide support to their users. It assumes the business partner has an AS/400 system and users have an AS/400 system, a System/36,

*or* a System/38. 50 *pp.*
