### 2\.1\.6 GC41\-9678, Publications Guide

The *Publications Guide* \(which you are currently using\) identifies and describes the printed and online information in the AS/400 library, as well as other publications about the AS/400 system\. It includes cross\-reference information between the current library and the previous version library, the System/36 library, and the System/38 library\. It also provides information about which publications are available with the product and describes how to order additional manuals\. *150 pp\., selfbound*

*Related Publications*: *Application System/400\* Library Structure*, S325\-6009; *System Introduction*, GC41\-9766

*Intended Audience*: All AS/400 users

*Distribution Information*: Included with program 5738\-SS1, feature 8001, in binder 0; $6\.00 for additional copies\.
