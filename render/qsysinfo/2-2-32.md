### 2\.2\.32 GG22\-9440, 8100/DOSF to AS/400 Office Migration Considerations

This publication provides those planning, coordinating, and implementing 8100/DOSF to AS/400 Office migration with an evaluation of DOSF functions and applications and comparable AS/400 Office functions\. It includes hints on migration planning, applications development, and application migration examples\. *125 pp\.*
