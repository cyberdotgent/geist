### 2\.2\.67 GG24\-3449, AS/400 Communications Definition Examples

This manual includes consistent definition examples which will help you to incorporate the AS/400 system in communications networks\. It is for communication professionals who are in charge of defining connections between AS/400 systems and systems such as System/370, System/36, System/38, Personal System/2, and terminal controllers\. *450 pp\.*
