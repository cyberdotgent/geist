### 2\.2\.31 GG22\-9428, PC Connections to AS/400 Office

This manual describes how AS/400 Office Release 3 may be accessed when various connectivity methods are employed\. The programmable work station, DOS and OS/2 Extended Edition V1\.2 based, is the main focus; however, nonprogrammable terminals are also addressed where appropriate\. The information clarifies when and how AS/400 Office \(including the editor\) may be accessible by a work station and what level of function the editor provides\. This publication obsoletes GG22\-9428\-00\. *50 pp\.*
