### 2\.1\.48 SC24\-5553, Programming: Procedures Language 400/REXX Programmer's Guide

*Publication Description*: *Procedures Language \(REXX\) Programmer's Guide* provides a wide\-ranging discussion of programming with REXX on the AS/400 system\. Its primary purpose is to provide useful programming information and examples to those who are new to Procedures Language 400/REXX and to provide those who have used REXX in other computing environments with information about the Procedures Language 400/REXX implementation\. *250 pp\.*

*Related Publications*: *Programming: Procedures Language 400/REXX Reference*, SC24\-5552

*Intended Audience*: System programmer

*Distribution Information*: Included with program 5738\-SS1, feature 8016, in binder 36; $25\.00 for additional copies\.
