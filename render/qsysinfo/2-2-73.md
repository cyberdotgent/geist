### 2.2.73 GG24-3501, AS/400 Security and Auditing Considerations

This publication supports AS/400 security and auditing considerations for release 2. The fundamentals of security for AS/400 systems as single systems and AS/400 systems running communications applications, PC Support/400 and AS/400 Office are considered. This publication would be helpful to systems engineers who advise on aspects of the AS/400 system, customers needing to implement sound security practices, and those

auditing such installations. *225* *pp.*
