#### 1\.2\.4\.1 Tutorial System Support

This is a basic course on AS/400 concepts such as *System Security Concepts* and *Working with System Displays* that is delivered with each system\. The course is tailored to specific user needs and is available to all system users, no matter what work station they are using\.
