### 2\.2\.51 GG24\-3268, AS/400 Office in a IIA/SNADS Network

This manual provides systems engineers and system administrators with information and examples about implementing distribution and library services on the AS/400 system\. The manual also evaluates various types of communications and discusses required administration steps\. *200 pp\.*
