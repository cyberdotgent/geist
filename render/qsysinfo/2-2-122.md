### 2.2.122 SV32-0616, AS/400:  a Programmer's View

This videotape explains the concepts that are basic to writing and maintaining programs on the AS/400 system. Two programmers introduce four key concepts: System Architecure Programming Tools System Management Connectivity Animated graphics help illustrate the concepts. The videotape is a preview for the AS/400 concepts manual. It is designed primarily for new AS/400 programmers who are not familiar with the System/38. This videotape may not be viewed using videotape players other than those for 1/2-inch VHS SECAM format videotape. Other formats of this videotape are available with order numbers: SV32-0609, 1/2-inch VHS NTSC US and
