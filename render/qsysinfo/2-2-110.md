### 2\.2\.110 SV32\-0602, Office Overview and Enrollment

This videotape provides information on how an OfficeVision/400 user can do many of the office functions\. These functions include working with mail and calendar information, and word processing\. There is also a segment for office administrators on enrolling AS/400 office users\. *1/2 inch, VHS, NTSC, 33 minutes\.*
