### 2.2.66 GG24-3447, AS/400 in Large Networks:  a Case Study

This manual describes scenarios involving IBM AS/400 systems in large networks. Included are evaluations of the feasibility of implementing those scenarios by indicating possible limitations and suggesting solutions to problems that arise. This manual is for communications specialists and those implementing a communications network who have an advanced knowledge of Systems Network Architecture (SNA) and network

management. *200* *pp.*
