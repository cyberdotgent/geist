### 1\.2\.7 Technical Information Exchange

Technical information exchange allows you to exchange files between your system and an IBM marketing support system\. You can receive a file on your system from an IBM marketing support system\. You can also send data from your system to an IBM marketing support system\.

To use technical information exchange:

1\. Type the Work with Technical Information Exchange \(WRKTIE\) command on any command line\. 2\. Press F4 \(Prompt\)\.
