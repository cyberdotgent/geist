### 2\.2\.109 SV31\-3405, Managing Your AS/400 Tape Library

This videotape explains the importance of three key items that can help ensure successful operations on the AS/400 tape drives\. The three areas that are discussed are proper tape handling, importance of tape labeling, and how the AS/400 system can help evaluate tapes\. It also includes quick problem determination steps to help users decide if the problems are caused by media or hardware\. *1/2 inch, VHS, NTSC, 18 minutes\.*
