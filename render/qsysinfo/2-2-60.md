### 2.2.60 GG24-3430, VM - AS/400 Connectivity and Functional Use

This manual provides information on the different products and connectivity possibilities that IBM provides to allow a VM system to be connected to an AS/400 system. It is intended to help VM specialists understand the capabilities of the various products in terms of functionality and connectivity. *200* *pp.*
