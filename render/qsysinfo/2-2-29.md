### 2\.2\.29 GC31\-6810, Systems Application Architecture Common Communications Support Summary

This manual provides those who develop or adapt a product to attach to an IBM network with an overview of the Systems Application Architecture \(SAA\) Common Communications Support \(CCS\)\. The manual summarizes the elements of CCS, which specifies protocols for interconnecting SAA systems and interchanging data among those systems\. However, this manual does not describe product implementations of CCS\. *100 pp\.*
