### 2\.2\.36 GG22\-9444, AS/400 Distributed Systems Implementation Guide, Volume 4

This manual outlines a number of considerations important when implementing NetView\* DM and AS/400 Distributed Systems Node Executive \(DSNX\) in a System/370 host\-controlled and centrally managed environment\. It is intended for the project manager or lead administrator responsible for the installation and implementation of NetView DM for the purpose of performing change management in a network of AS/400 systems\. Knowledge of basic AS/400 systems operations and facilities and basic knowledge of NetView DM and OS/400 DSNX is necessary\. Topics included are an overview of NetView DM and DSNX, AS/400 operational considerations for management examples, and information on problem determination\. *75 pp\.*
