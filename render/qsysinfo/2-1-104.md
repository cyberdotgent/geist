### 2\.1\.104 SC41\-9626, Systems Application Architecture\* OfficeVision/400\*: Planning

*For and Setting Up OfficeVision/400*

*Publication Description*: *Planning For and Setting Up OfficeVision/400\** provides information about planning for and setting up OfficeVision/400\. The information includes planning for enrolling users, word processing, mail and calendar processing, using OfficeVision/400 with IBM personal computers, and using OfficeVision/400 in a communications network\. The planning activities include filling out planning worksheets that are used to do the setup tasks\. *225 pp\.*

*Related Publications* *Systems Application Architecture\* OfficeVision/400\*: Managing OfficeVision/400*, SC41\-9627

*Intended Audience*: Office administrator

*Distribution Information*: Included with program 5738\-WP1, in binder 49; $10\.00 for additional copies\.
