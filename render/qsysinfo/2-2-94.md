### 2\.2\.94 SC09\-1221, System/36\-to\-AS/400 Work Station Utility Conversion Guide

This publication provides applications programmers with information about converting work station utility programs on System/36 to RPG III programs on the AS/400 system\. It offers suggested techniques and examples in hardcopy form and on the diskette provided\. *150 pp\.*
