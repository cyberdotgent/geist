### 2\.2\.27 GC26\-4531, Concepts of AD/Cycle

This manual provides business and data processing professionals with information to help evaluate IBM's AD/Cycle and begin preliminary planning for its implementation\. It contains an overview of AD/Cycle, a description of the conceptual elements of AD/Cycle, a general discussion of its open framework, and suggestions on how to prepare for AD/Cycle\. However, this manual does not include information about programming interfaces for customers\. *50 pp\.*
