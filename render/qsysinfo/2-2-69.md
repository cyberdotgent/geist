### 2\.2\.69 GG24\-3464, OfficeVision/400 Application Programming Interfaces

This manual describes the AS/400 Office application programming interfaces \(API\) and gives examples of integrating the OfficeVision/400 licensed program with business applications\. This publication is meant to be a reference guide for programmers and analysts who want to integrate OfficeVision/400 with their business applications\. *200 pp\.*
