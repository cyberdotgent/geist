#### 1\.2\.4\.3 Other Online Education

You may also use AS/400 online education to present courses that were not developed by IBM\. These can be courses written by other companies or courses you have developed yourself using SEDF/2\. These courses can cover any topic and fulfill a wide range of educational needs\.

To install a course purchased from a company other than IBM, see the instructions that accompany the course\. To install a course you developed yourself using SEDF/2, see the *Self Education Facility on the Personal System/2\*: Author's Guide*, SH12\-5623\.
